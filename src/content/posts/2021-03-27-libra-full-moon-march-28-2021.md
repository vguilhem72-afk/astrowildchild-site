The Moon shines its full light in the sign of Libra. Being one of the more classier signs of the zodiac, Libra’s refined nature stems from looking for the ideal, desiring harmony by seeing things from above and on equal grounds. An energy that advocates balance and beauty, looks for pleasure in the world around them. With tensions likely flaring up in relationships, issues likely occurring with material resources and in the things we find physically pleasurable, keeping that ego in check will help. With Saturn in Aquarius making a harmonious link to the Full Moon, there is an added clarity of thought and duty. It’s an energy that supports a broader outlook and that infamous Libra grace, which should help point toward solutions to keep the scales from tipping off kilter.
