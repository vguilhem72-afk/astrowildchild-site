![](/images/uploads/2021/05/Jupiter4-640x897.png)New hope, wisdom and opportunities shine down on all of us, as the greater benefic Jupiter enters into its domicile sign of Pisces.

Compared to Jove’s transit in the two previous signs, where its propensity to expand was weakened, limited in Capricorn and neutralized in Aquarius, Jupiter bursts in strength of optimism, inspiration, and generosity in the energy of the fish. Now in its home sign, it’s a blending of compatible energy, allowing for the sky god’s best qualities to shine through.

Jupiter shows us where we can grow, spread generosity, have tolerance and faith, it outlines our beliefs and shades the color to our vision of the future and how we can be inspired to go after it. In the expression of Pisces, beliefs lean towards the mystical, beyond 3d reality, a stretch into alternate realms.The energy of the all-seeing eye, or better yet, the all sensing element. Being that it is the last sign of the zodiac, there is ancient wisdom, divine knowledge found in the vast oceans of the Pisces waters. A celestial understanding of the intrinsic nature of life and the connection to all.

Those with a natal Jupiter Pisces are defined by compassion, visions of love and unity. They usually find joy when swimming in their imagination and creativity. A base to facilitate their sense of purpose and meaning to sway with the currents of life and the flexible, complex and boundless energy on which existence thrives.

Influence on a Collective and Personal Level

On a collective level, we can tap into the transit to restore faith where it is needed, soften or erase some of the separation and isolation some of us might have endured and continue to feel by threading compassion, trust and love to connect us. On a personal level, we can source the well-spring of renewed optimism, envision new horizons in at least one area of our life. Making optimal use of Jupiter’s wisdom in Pisces will be about summoning our own divine powers of imagination and creativity, along wth compassion, forgiveness and love to unify what has been lost or gone astray.

Awareness and Accountability

Both Jupiter and Pisces share the qualities of tolerance, expansion and idealism, which makes for a double dosage of impact. The divine and ancient wisdom of Pisces brought down to earth, from realms beyond our own reality plane, can easily turn into naïveté, escapism, delusions, self-deceptions, distracting from purpose and truth. Consequently, a spiralling down into irrational waters, false, grandiose beliefs and skewed insight, where we can be swept away by tides of confusion. Having awareness can temper the currents, while accountability can gather the patch of oceanic pollution.

Awareness can be gained and accountability can be claimed with the aspects—both harmonious and challenging—transit  Jupiter makes with its cosmic neighbours. To note, Jove makes no major aspects to other outer planets until 2022 when it blends in harmonious energy with Uranus in Taurus—inspiring new levels of connection. However, we can still benefit and keep the cogs of personal evolution spiralling upwards from the speedier transits to personal planets.

Other Ways to Avoid Being Completely Seduced by the Mystical Influence

We can always look to the opposite energy of natal and transit Saturn, to ground us in reality. We can also be neutralized by any of our own earthy planets and houses or resort to parts of life that help keep things bound to our five senses.

Wherever this is happening for each of us, the two-year long transit (with retrograde transits involved) will shine down wisdom of letting go, to trust what may lay beyond reality, to give faith in the unseen. That hope, connectivity and inspiration may be found in our powers of creativity and visions of the ideal. An opportunity to practice surrendering to something bigger, to the intricate flow of life.

Jupiter in Pisces is the closing of a 12-year cycle around the Sun, associated with beliefs and the wisdom accumulated. What has been shaped, integrated, what we are absolving and dissolving can be the bridge towards a refreshed vision for the future, trail-blazed from the fires of Jupiter in Aries, lit up with exciting novel adventures and a new cycle of blessings.
