## **Venus in October and November 2026: It'a all connected.**

There is a system at work right now and [Venus ](https://astrowildchild.com/blog/venus-in-astrology/)is at the center of it. Not because she chose to be but because every planet connected to her story is demanding something from her simultaneously. Mars, Pluto, Uranus, Chiron, Mercury, the North Node, the South Node. All of them feeding into the same requirement: transform or stay stuck. There is no third option.

To understand what November is offering you have to understand what October put Venus through and why.

**The Ecosystem**

What makes this period so intense is not any single transit. It is the interconnection between all of them. Mars is the thread that runs through everything. Mars rules Scorpio, the sign Venus has been traveling through. Mars rules Aries, the sign Chiron is currently sitting in. Mars is in Leo, the sign of the South Node. And Mars opposes Pluto in Aquarius while squaring Venus. One planet governing three critical points in the sky. That is not coincidence. That is an ecosystem. Every piece feeds the others. Every tension in one area amplifies tension in another.

Mercury is in Scorpio too, retrograde, excavating the subconscious mind while Venus excavates the heart. Uranus in Gemini sends an inconjunct to Venus, creating restlessness and sudden disruption in the areas of life she governs. Pluto in Aquarius squares her from the sign of collective evolution. Nothing is isolated here. The love life affects the finances. The finances affect the self-worth. The self-worth affects the identity. The identity affects the relationships. It is a closed loop of transformation and every part of it is being activated at once.

### **What October Did**

When Venus entered Scorpio she walked into a house full of mirrors she did not want to look into. The squares to Pluto in Aquarius hit on September 15th and again on October 19th. Both times the same message: be honest about what is not working in love, money, self-worth, beauty and pleasure. Pluto does not ask gently. Pluto strips. Pluto exposes. And in Aquarius, Pluto does it for the sake of collective growth, evolution beyond the personal. What is real and honest does not just serve you. It serves everyone around you.

Mars in Leo squared Venus at the same time, adding a layer of ego, pride and confrontation. But Mars in Leo is not just aggression. At its highest expression Mars in Leo is about expressing yourself unapologetically. Being who you are without anybody’s approval. An acceptance of self so grounded that it inspires others by the confident way you carry it. That is what Mars in Leo wants from Venus. Stop hiding. Stop brooding in suspicion. Stop waiting for permission to be seen.

Venus in Scorpio tends to shy away from the spotlight. She holds back, observes, tests, guards. There is wisdom in that but there is also fear. Fear of being exposed. Fear of vulnerability being used against her. And when Pluto and Mars both pressure her at once, what surfaces is everything she has been protecting herself from. The buried insecurities around worth. The unspoken resentments in relationships. The financial patterns rooted in scarcity instead of abundance. The beauty she hides because being seen feels dangerous.

Both of her main enemies in October were ruler planets. Mars rules the sign she is traveling through. Pluto is the modern ruler of Scorpio. In other words, Venus was not fighting external forces. She was fighting herself. Going against your own ruling energy is like arguing with your reflection. You cannot win because the opposition is you. The only resolution is to stop fighting and start integrating. What do these parts of you actually need? What buried truth, if confronted deeply, would free up love, money, self-worth and pleasure?

**The Point of It**

There is always a point with the outer planets. Pluto wants transformation. Uranus wants liberation. The North Node in Aquarius wants growth toward something larger than personal comfort. Leo’s South Node wants you to release the ego patterns, the need for validation, the pride that blocks genuine connection. All of these converge on Venus and say the same thing: evolve. Upgrade how you love. Upgrade how you earn. Upgrade how you see yourself. Upgrade what you allow yourself to enjoy.

Aquarius is growth. Leo is confidence and security in identity. Together on the nodal axis they ask: can you be genuinely confident without needing applause? Can you grow toward something meaningful without dragging your ego along? Venus in Scorpio’s journey through October was preparing you to answer those questions honestly. The squares were not punishment. They were excavation. Digging up what was buried so you could see it clearly enough to release it.

Some form of death is happening either way. That is the nature of this ecosystem. Whether you work with it consciously or resist it entirely, something is dying. A relationship pattern. A financial habit. A belief about your worth. A way of hiding your beauty. The only question is whether the death is a transformation you chose or a destruction you suffered because you refused to choose.

#### **What November Offers**

Venus retrogrades back into Libra around October 25th and stays there for the rest of November, stationing direct in the sign of balance, harmony, grace and consideration. After the raw intensity of Scorpio, Libra is softer. More receptive. The energy shifts from excavation to reconciliation.

As soon as Venus enters Libra she trines the North Node in Aquarius. This is the fulfilling path opening up. After everything October uncovered, November shows you where the promising growth is for Venus in both the Scorpio and Libra parts of your chart. The trine says: this direction leads somewhere genuinely good. Not comfortable in the old way. Good in a way that reflects who you are becoming.

Venus also sextiles both Mars and Jupiter in Leo. The same Mars that was squaring her and creating friction in October is now supporting her. The same fiery energy that felt like an attack transforms into motivation, confidence and hope. Jupiter adds optimism, warmth and the sense that brighter days are ahead. Mars and Jupiter together say: you can move forward with courage and grace simultaneously. They do not have to compete.

November is for making amends. For releasing the South Node in Leo patterns of ego, pride and the need to be right. For moving forward into a more genuinely gratifying path. Every square and opposition from October was preparing you for this moment. The lighter you walk into November the more you can receive from it.

##### **Chiron’s Final Invitation**

Chiron in Aries opposes Venus in Libra throughout this period. Old wounds around identity, self-worth and relationships surface one more time. But this is not a reopening of wounds for the sake of pain. This is Chiron offering a final chance to soothe, to make peace, to integrate what has been fractured between your sense of self and your relationships.

Chiron will eventually leave Aries for Taurus and will not return to the Aries part of your chart for decades. Whatever healing is available in the Aries and Libra axis of your chart right now is time-sensitive. The wounds that have defined how you show up in partnerships, how you assert your identity, how you balance self with other — this is the window to address them with the grace Venus in Libra provides.

**The Invitation**

We are all being given a chance to make things better. Not through force. Not through control. Not through the Scorpio instinct to grip tighter. Through grace. Through balance. Through the willingness to release what is dead and trust that what replaces it will be more honest, more fulfilling and more aligned with who you are actually becoming.

The ecosystem that built the pressure is the same ecosystem offering the resolution. Mars, Pluto, Uranus, Chiron, the nodes. They are not separate forces working against you. They are one interconnected system asking the same question from every angle: are you ready to evolve?

Venus says yes. The question is whether you will follow her.
