This Full Moon Eclipse in Gemini can have us bouncing in different emotional directions. An Eclipse can bring about unwanted changes, bring us to a final stage in life and usually gifts us insight on what was previously unknown. All of which can stretch our minds in different directions, into confusion and uncertainty. Grounding ourselves to a calmer state can be about looking through an objective lens, while letting go and allowing the forces of the cosmos to do its job. In other words, if a road has ended, if changes occur, aligning in the best way possible can start to pave more stimulating paths. Where we can bounce in child-like wonder and excitement and kick up our heels down endless possibilities.
