Similar to the breath we take, stars shine bright and twinkle in radiance. Life is much sweeter when there is little effort to express who you are. It requires knowing yourself by understanding what you want and value, which can sometimes fade and become out of focus in life’s messiness and complications. Your natal Venus can help restore you back to a healthy balance.

It if is in the 4th House, the comfort of home always does one good. If it is in the 11th, friends and social circles are the ideal ways to find more joy in life. In the 1st House, delight is found in the self and what we need to experience to feel the joys of being in our own skin. It is a placement that allows real focus on how we see things, which ultimately colours our perception and informs our relationships to others and the world around.

By transit, Venus in the first House usually gives us a taste of that every year for about a month. But if you’re looking for a more in-depth experience or can’t wait for its annual return in that part of your chart, consider getting a relocation chart reading to see which part of the world has Venus in your 1st House to get that boost of Goddess-like status and feel the love that we should always give to ourselves.

Having this placement natally is a gift to the senses. One has an inherent sense of self-value and confidence. Through lived experiences, the native truly appreciates oneself and their position in the world. Get ready to feel blissful in your skin with a healthy self-esteem boost. The experience can show you the value of self-value. The _hows_ will depend on the sign being expressed in Venus.

### What you can expect when you have Venus relocated in your **1st House of Self**

**Focus on You**

The main objective is to turn your energy and focus on you to understand your needs and what feels good. Generally speaking, preferences and ideals are clearly separated from others. It’s a perception re-evaluation, updating what isn’t up to par. It helps you start to merit your time and efforts, cutting out what isn’t necessary any longer (without the guilt—if that applies) You begin to appreciate yourself and prioritize time with whom and what exactly deserves that. You begin to love yourself all over again.

**Values and Needs Clear**

Your love for yourself equates to clear values and needs, slating as the foundation of your minute decisions. With Venus in the 1st House, you feel comfortable asking for what you want.

Depending on your stage in life, your values recharge you and can reorient your focus. You put shape into your day, your relationships and life in general to enjoy more of what you want. To feel more fulfilled.

For example, if you have a natal 12th house Venus, issues separating wants and needs from others are common.

10th House Venus Pisces may have problems asking for a raise or changing careers and positions.

8th House Venus may get lost in the give-and-take dynamic, in the extremes of giving too much at one point and/or taking too much at another. Power struggles with an 8th House Venus can bring awareness when placed in the 1st House.

**![](/images/uploads/2023/03/3-640x640.png)Living in the Moment Through Your Goddess-like Senses**

Your 1st House Venus in a relocation gives you an immediate sense of your goddess stature by caring about your presence in both physical attributes and energy radiation. You feel good; pleasure is felt when you maintain yourself on both levels.

The energy radiates from a direct stream of self-awareness through the values you uphold and the needs expressed at the forefront.

You find grace in maintaining a deity’s physical beauty through your inherent and individuated feminity. A beauty routine helps you refine and maintain what that means for you. It feels less of a chore or obligation, with the pleasures of your senses activated and your sense of aesthetics heightened. The good feeling it gives turns into appreciation.

Attending to both aspects of self is what creates divine feelings. When you find comfort in your own skin, momentary pleasures are made. It is in this blissful state which stimulates the flow and connection to others and the earth. In confidence and a natural sense of being, like the trees or the birds, you have a true sense of belonging. A right to be here.

Creating clear boundaries, more balance and learning to say NO THANK YOU.

**Practice Healthy Selfishness**

Asserting your needs, wants, and desires is not selfish. It gives you and others clarity and healthy boundaries. When you express healthy boundaries, you are practicing self-love. You are clear on what you want and understand that it is okay to say no. Venus relocation in the 1st House experience helps you know what you have power over and don’t. It enables you to glide in rhythm.

**Consistency Creates Balance**

Venus can sometimes get indulgent when values and wants are not clear. The other side could be giving too much. The power of Venus is about her reciprocal exchanges; she represents balance —think of Libra scales. When you don’t know what YOU want, the scales can drop out of kilter. The inherent sense of equilibrium she represents will consequently slide to compensation. You can tilt from too much giving at one period or taking at another. Venus in the 1st House gives you awareness and practice to balance out tendencies of selfishness and practice to understand the give-and-take benefits.

Venus in your 1st house and the values you uphold and preferences asserted enable consistency to create a balance between a healthy relationship with yourself, others and the world around.

**Power of Attraction**

Once you have all the above worked out, practicing it as you go about life, you learn to understand your power of attraction. This is the main component and benefit to Venus in the 1st house. Think of celebrities like Angelina Jolie. A visibly stunning women who defines the stature of Venus and beauty. How often have you heard other stars (primarily men of her calibre–in a way, gods in their own right, like Johnny Depp or Clint Eastwood) talk about AJ and her illuminating qualities? She also exudes a charisma of confidence. She radiates star power through awareness and refinement of her physical beauty.

With Venus in your 1st house by relocation, you begin to understand your star quality. It is like no other feeling and has nothing to do with being arrogant or acting superior to others; instead, through your understanding of self-love and practicing healthy boundaries, you ooze in attractive, glowing confidence. A natural magnetism you radiate through self-realization and clarity.

**Takeaway**

If you have Venus in a less than favourable house, a 1st house Venus in a relocation chart can help you boost your confidence and self-worth. It takes an awareness of your needs and values while practicing them through your senses and environment to get you into the full figure of a Goddess-like stature.

Aries Venus in the 1st House can help balance values of feminine qualities vs. masculine sensibility and how stepping back and being less aggressive can attract what you want.

**Venus in Taurus** — A nice one to have, it can help balance indulgence with your senses, food and its comforts. Like the Aries Venus, this placement can have you more selfish than usual. But if you act from the expression’s highest vibration, you stay in the moment, indulge in each moment, balancing what you need. You feel your own body, the glut and the scarcity. The awareness helps you focus on your relationship outside of yourself and what you are radiating.

**Venus in Gemini**— It’s okay to change what you want. The flow is in variety, exchange and interaction. Look out for wanting it all, instability and FOMO on the bounty Venus offers in the sign of Gemini. With the 1st house, you can benefit from feeling your senses ground you in the reality of your skin, tongue, eyes…which can clear the chaos and slow down the business of your mind. Gemini

Venus wants to impress by taking on the energy of others to keep things light. You can continue to keep things light and airy by flowing in the moment through breathwork, taking in the pleasures of what the earth has to offer. Venus in the house of self can help you slow down.

**Venus in Cancer** — You’re probably the least likely to relocate since you are all about comfort and home. The relocation Venus or vacation can help you connect with self-nurturance and self-preservation. Expect to experience a desire for self-care via reorienting physical and internal values. Lessons of self-acceptance and the need to separate your wants through re-examining values will benefit you. A clarity that enables you to give that much more love and quality time to others.

**Venus in Leo**— Accepting yourself to shine as you come naturally. Having Venus in your 1st house gives you that breathing room to be yourself again and radiate the charisma and heart with which you are meant to warm others. If the imbalance is the opposite, this placement can help you get in touch with what you really want, how you want to shine without dimming someone else’s light.

When you focus on yourself and your values, that confident fire can spread even brighter to inspire others to do the same. But sometimes, it can be stained by certain obligations you might have, roles you have to play in life that dim your true Leo light.

**Venus in Virgo** — You can have difficulty floating into a relationship. It can come from a deep insecurity of not being enough so you project that onto others and criticize them for their flaws. Venus in Virgo naturally sees the flaws; they are the discerners sifting the wheat from the shaft. Which is healthy when there is mindfulness and when values are clear. When Venus is in your 1st house by relocation, you may start to experience your own weaknesses and why. Awareness of just how critical you are is the start of a healthier foundation with self and others. It is a balance of helping and discerning what isn’t helping you to achieve satisfaction.

**Venus in Libra** — A strong placement that can tip either way in the scales. Pushing selfish agenda can cause conflict with the sign that naturally wants to achieve balance. By having Venus in your first house, you can align yourself again to what you want, to focus on what is essential and ideal. **Venus in Scorpio** —Involving Venus themes, extremes are likely integral experiences throughout your life. Scorpio is a sign about relationships on a deep level. You want and expect the same. By having Venus in your 1st house, you surface from the depths of Scorpio waters to breathe and feel the air, the sun, and your senses. **Venus in Sagittarius** — You can spread yourself too thin regarding your desires. Your lens is wide. You want it all and are willing to get it all.
