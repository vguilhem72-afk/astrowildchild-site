> We Are All The Signs

We all know our Sun sign, some of us are also familiar with our Moon and Rising sign.  What you may not know is that we are all the signs, some being more accentuated than others. Your Sun sign maybe in Leo and your Moon in Scorpio, with a Rising in Gemini. We also include the rest of our native planets, Mercury, Mars and Venus. As well as, the outer planets—Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune and Pluto. There’s also important asteroids, fixed stars and heavenly points—including Chiron, Regulus & the North Node, to name a few.

Whatever happens up there, happens down here. _**“As above, so below.”**_

![](/images/uploads/2019/06/audrey-kawasaki-02.jpg)

Each celestial body or point represent a different part of ourselves, working to create an integrated whole. We express each element in different shades & capacities.

When it comes to the zodiac signs themselves, its affliction or harmonious state determines our potential to express the energy in a negative or positive light.

An afflicted Mars in Pisces may be driven to alcoholic tendencies or lack passionate motivation. On the flip, when it is vibrating on high currents, the action planet can be inspired by spiritual pursuits or charitable causes. Mercury joys in Virgo and can express the best of its energy through communication and analysis of thought, but on the low, can become too critical & cynical, solely relying on cold facts and reason. Again, this all depends on the dynamics within your astrological birth chart.

Energy is never static. The blueprint of our natal chart shows us our weaknesses, challenges, along with our highest potentials and gifts. We can be ineffective, act within our limitations or level up & be empowered.

**What does your chart look like? How healthy is it?**
