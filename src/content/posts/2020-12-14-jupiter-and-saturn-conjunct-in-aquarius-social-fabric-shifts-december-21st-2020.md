**The cycle between Jupiter and Saturn Conjunction**

Jupiter and Saturn will both be moving into the tribe sign Aquarius. Blending their quality of energy in air element after 240 years in earth signs. Jupiter takes a revolution of 12 years around the Sun, while Saturn takes a revolution of 30 years. Jupiter in its speed will bypass the sun once before meeting again with Saturn in another sign in the same element eight years later. It will take sixty years to make a complete trigon(the same three elements). Referred to as the TRANSFER OF THE TRANSIT in another element, the conjunction involves a cycle through each triplicity four times, before moving into another element. The unique, impactful event on the 21st of December trumping the previous conjunctions.

The evolution of the importance of the Saturn and Jupiter conjunction dates back to the Babylonian dynasties of Mesopotamia, before Greek astronomers created their tables of cosmic activities.  The study of the celestial sky progressed down the channels of history from one era to another, diverted in focus based on shifts in ruling empires. (Omens were once based on the planetary activity of Venus.) Revering the rest of the major planetary bodies in the following era, which established celestial coordinates, a more accurate analysis of planetary motion. At which point combined the main paradigms of science, religion and astrology at the time, particularly the Zoroastrian-Persian ideology. Theories and works that were found in the Book of Conjunctions, belonging to volumes of astrological work written by an elite Persian astrologer and astronomer named Abu Ma’shar. His works were considered the most influential to Islamic astrology at the time. Important events were linked to the Jupiter and Saturn conjunction, namely the coming of two prophets Muhammad and Christ. However, 15 centuries later, it was Johannes Kepler, the Modern Father of Astronomy and a brilliant astrologer, that studied the reoccurring patterns of conjunction for Jupiter and Saturn. He discovered that the Star of Bethlehem aligned with Jupiter and Saturn conjunction in Pisces.
