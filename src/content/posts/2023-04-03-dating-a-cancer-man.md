No matter what planet the person has in Cancer, its intensity will be determined by that planet and the overall health of the chart. Thus, also determining the frequency of higher or lower expression.

### **General Cancer Energy**

Dating A Cancer Man and What to Expect, Why You Should Stay Away

Unless severely traumatized by the past, the higher Cancer expression is usually felt (even if subtly) with male Cancers. It is in their cardinal make-up to share who and what they are. In other words, quite expressive emotionally, one can usually sense their state of being. They are not afraid of their emotions, having a sixth sense of their strength and power to connect and help others. It is their innate language of connection to speak in compassion and sympathy. In its highest expression, a Cancer male will want to make you feel loved and welcomed.

#### **Rising in Cancer**

Our rising sign reveals how we experience the world. It is our lookout point, colored by the signs and planets in the 1st House. Because Cancers are a sponge to the environment,  they initially suss you out for potential threats. Tradition, home, and qualities related to mother come with the territory for this sensitive bunch. The actions taken by a Rising Cancer male will be informed by his Mars energy. It will add to his sympathetic and compassionate nature if it is in a fellow water sign. If it is in air or earth, his approach turns rational, which can ground his emotions —even contain them for self-protection. Fire adds spontaneity but can mean highly combustible emotions.

Chivalry is the highest expression for a Rising Cancer male. His natural outlook is to protect. Whether that be themselves or others they care about. He is the natural gatekeeper of the home. A Cancer Rising man is an inward energy with the soft touches of a nurturing mother.

##### **Mars**

Men tend to express their Mars personality at the initial encounter. Knowing a man’s Mars energy can help you understand what he is attracted to sexually, his desires and how he acts on them. Mars represents his carnal, instinctual nature. It is his Mars that fights for what he wants.

The House it sits in and the sign expressed describe what quenches his thirst. A Mars in Cancer is the protector of his abode. Mars is a masculine energy. In the sign of Cancer, his aggression is soften but he will know when it is necessary to raise his pincers. The potency of an intuitive Mars is emotional expression, a revealed and comfortable vulnerability.  The combination of cardinal energy makes for a purposeful Mars. He will be invigorated by self-initiative to conquer the dynamic world.

**Warning:** When dating a Cancer Man– He can become the snappy emotional crab if he doesn’t have what he wants. This is a Mars that will express different emotional shades.

Depending on your own Mars and considering your Venus, it will either temper or inflame his Mars energy. Desires include a comfortable home– he is a creature of comfort. Creating a family will be part of his drive. Finding a perfect mate to share in his desire adds fuel to his drive.

In the bedroom, he will caress you with sweet, warm touches. Foreplay may start with food, a massage or anything that will lather the senses to spike. A Cancer Mars isn’t known for his aggression, rather for his romantic love notes, gentle touches and considerate nature to make you feel satisfied.

###### **Jupiter**

Jupiter is also a significant factor in getting to know your person. The qualities of Jupiter are thought to be the ideal husband for a woman. However, generally speaking, it can summarize someone’s belief system. Ideally, you want to have similar outlooks and an overall compatible mission. Jupiter in fire is in natural conflict with Cancer energy. But can also inspire optimism and light the fire of creativity. Jupiter in the air sign can outline and strengthen connectivity for Cancer energy. Earth Jupiter can help sustain a more grounded view for the emotional traps that Cancer can find itself treading.

Jupiter is exalted in Cancer. If you have this in your chart, you will be attracted to the qualities a Cancer man expresses.

Family is central in a Jupiter in Cancer. They view what Cancer represents as significant for a relationship’s strong foundation and healthy growth. If family is part of your life plans, if spending time with origin of family is on top of your list, if settling down in one place to root is important, a man with Cancer Jupiter beliefs will bode well for you.

**Sun**

In modern times, the Sun serves neutral influence on both men and women. It can also represent the mother for some. However, males and females can have significant differences when it comes to their Sun qualities. For example, if we were to assign the Sun to the father in two different charts of siblings, one being a female and the other being male, both can have different points of view of their fathers based on gender. The male will see the father figure he is to become, identifying to similar gender aspects. The father’s influence might be the physical strength and protection he shows and provides for the family or lack thereof.

The son will grow into his Sun based on his gender bias compared to his sister.  She sees a father figure perhaps in a similar light and could possibly inherit what she sees growing up, but the main difference being she will also look for it in her potential mate. Generally, there is a kind of separation or integration based on physical gender. There are, however, many factors to consider whether her Sun dominates over her Moon. (Her Moon can represent her father too!)

Sun in Cancer Man can generally take on the more ‘masculine’ side of the energy. He may lean on the more entrepreneurial side and want to delegate finances. A Cancer Sun man will, however, have a naturally sensitive disposition and may have a masculine view of when to express sensitivity. The idea that there is an appropriate place for everything may ring true for him.

**Venus**

We can get a broader view when we look at his Venus. Venus rules his wants, what he is attracted to, his personal likes and dislikes, for which his five senses desire. His Venus qualities give him the spark in his eyes.

Venus in Fire signs will offset or create more emotional flux in him. The watery Venus will spike his sensitivity, giving him a tendency to be more covert about his feelings. Earthy Venus will keep him patient, subtle, and observant. He can express his views, show words of affection readily in the air signs.

The Venus in Cancer is the matriarch of the family. He may be looking for that in his mate. Someone who can express that motherly love or at least the attentive nature of the archetype. He is looking for someone to make a home with him and expand his progeny. His ideal mate is feminine, will take the helm in his personal life. Like a fluffy cushion, or supple breasts and arms, he scopes out someone who can comfortably embrace him into a gentle reverie.

**![Dating A Cancer Moon](/images/uploads/2023/04/6-640x640.png)Moon**

The Moon is important to all. The cultural conditioning/gender bias you have been shaped by is irrelevant. Our Moon sign is probably the most significant part of us. In the most personal area of life, the energy reveals our inner nature and what we need to survive and thrive emotionally. Our Moon sign needs are non-negotiable.

A man’s Moon can reveal how he was nurtured, mothered. It will reflect what he requires from his partner. How he will care for you. This is the love language(along with Venus) of his soul. The watery Moon will need emotional nurturance and amplify his Cancer energy. The earth type will help stabilize a moody Cancer energy. If his Moon is in an air sign, he can be more forthcoming about his feelings. The fire moon will stoke that compassionate and nurturing side.

Not being in touch or accepting this strong feminine aspect of himself is a shadowed Moon–a neglected part of himself.

If his moon is strongly afflicted and has bad aspects, he can be an emotional rut. Defined by his constant needs, battling life looking for a mother source. Hypersensitive to all, unable to discern rational behaviour and emotional tantrums, he can leave you high and dry when you require support, yourself, retreating from overwhelming turbulent waters. A reminder of their own unfulfilled needs.

The Moon is home in Cancer energy. It will glow in emotional expression glow and flow in its changing tides. A man with a  Cancer Moon can be deeply in touch with his needs and longing and finely attuned to your own.
