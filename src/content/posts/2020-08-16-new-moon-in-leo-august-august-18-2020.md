With four personal celestial bodies in fire sign influencing the waxing phase, this Leo new Moon is lit with fiery energy. The environment burns of creative, courageous & passionate force and what you plant, the cycle you start now has the potential to magnetize & grow into star quality power.

However, the air is also concentrated in tension. Other areas of life can impede the flow and inherently pollute new growth. Impurities of ego-based decisions, neglecting obligation and duty due to ill-considered individual drives are likely. Instant gratification can outweigh long-term satisfaction. Manipulative actions can splinter trust.

Without any airy elements, impulse can override logic and reason. Relating to others can also become challenging for some.

Whatever you plan to cultivate, slow your roll & consider the long term effects.

With the conflicting energies involved, use internal authority to ground thoughts and action. Save energy and consider the value of time. Forming a clear plan of action, sourcing depth of desire and wisdom during times of flux and uncertainty can help create solid, nourished grounds from which your intentions can bud, and grow into its most attractive, inspirational and heart-spirited expression. Happy New Moon in Leo!
