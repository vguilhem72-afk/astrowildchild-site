The Libra New Moon gives us all a chance to start fresh in at least one area of life. It’s a great opportunity to restore balance, create new perspective under the themes ruled by the Libra energy: relationships, communication and will.

Despite both being in detriment, Venus in Scorpio can help us sow intentions rooted in truth and what we truly desire. Mars in Libra can gives us the conscious, warrior-like spirit to see it through in full cultivation.  To offset the lower vibrations the two planets tend to express in their current respective signs, it is about awareness. Find the highest qualities of each element and merge them to its highest possibilities. Ex. Venus Scorpio — deep healing love, the truth in your desire. Mars in Libra: Seeing yourself as part of the equation or fighting for what is right.

The sky is lit in airy energy, giving us further cosmic support. With a Libra stellium, including Mercury Rx, we are ripe with ideas, strategies and charm. The positive aspect Mercury makes with Jupiter in Aquarius elevates our thoughts to higher paths, supporting the Librian energy of justice and equality.  Saturn in Aquarius trines Lilith and North Node in Gemini, giving us pointers toward what’s worth it, exciting and authentic.

What can shade us in the lower vibes of the Libra New Moon is the square to Aries Chiron, along with Capricorn Pluto turning direct while making a square to Libra Mercury Rx. Energies from certain areas of life can hold us back, infiltrate our ability to make firm decisions. This can involve outer obligations, insecurities about self-identity and what powers we hold. Being objective, graceful and cooperative, yet also frank and without discounting the raw truth can make for more peaceful and equal grounds. Thus, setting a nourishing environment for buds to sprout in ideal and harmonious growth. Happy New Moon in Libra!
