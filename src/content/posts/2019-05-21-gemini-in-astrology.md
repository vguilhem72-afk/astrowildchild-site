## Gemini in Astrology is the Messenger, Symbolic of the Twins, With One Foot in the Heavens and the Other Foot in the Mundane World.

## _**Gemini’s can get a bad rap.**_

![Gemini in Astrology Twin Symbol](/images/uploads/2019/05/2-copy.png)

The sign, famous for its curious, outgoing nature and quick-witted sensibility also gets flack for having a loose tongue and flitting from one thing to another. At its lowest vibration, it has the propensity to tell lies and become two-faced! Yikes!

Gemini is associated with the Messenger god Mercury, who in mythological stories delivered goods to the gods.  It is the messages or cargo that associated [Mercury](https://astrowildchild.com/astrology-elements/mercury-in-astrology-expression-and-interaction/) and Gemini with communication, the media, transportation and daily interactions. Think of postmen, bus drivers, administrators and communicators of varying levels.

As the messenger of the zodiac, it gives them the inherent qualities of swift physical movement, listening, talking and sharing. To get the job done, it’s vital to keep things objective, flexible, busy, fun and interesting. What happens when a Gemini keeps all that load of mail, all that information milling around its head? The feather-footed messenger’s sprite energy gets weighed down and congested. In time, the system becomes overloaded, turning into inner chaos, a mental whirlwind that dips to low-frequency energy.

In the symbolism of the twins, one represents the heavens and the other the mundane world, both in service to each realm. The highest vibration of Gemini is genuine service and remembering who they are by seeing and experiencing both sides. The higher wings of perspective keep them light, friendly and detached. They stay adaptable to daily engagements, in flux to the changing environment and the various stories encountered.
