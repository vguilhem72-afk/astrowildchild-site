## Pisces at the 10th House: Finding Clarity and Confidence in Career, Goals and Worldly Success

> The only rule you need to follow is the unruliness of your intuition. Float into what feels right.

![Pisces at the 10th House ](/images/uploads/2022/03/1.png)Imagine building a structure in space and while you float in zero-gravity existence, you’re constructing material pieces into a whole. The essentials include proper space tools and equipment to get the job done effectively, and efficiently. Similar to astronaut engineers repairing a spacecraft in space, a Pisces at the cusp of the 10th is like taking a spacewalk to build and create a career. It requires being suited up in intuition, take proper breaks for creative inspiration and have tethers to keep tools intact and from floating out into the abyss.

10th House

To reach the apex of 10th house goals and career, envisioning, planning and follow-through are the chief steps to get there. Along with skill and ultimate mastery to maintain an elevated position, often it takes perseverance and discipline to overcome setbacks and obstacles. In other words, like the symbolism of the Mountain Goat scaling success requires a game plan to navigate the rough terrain, sticking to it, no matter how rocky and risky.

On the opposite end of that grit and grime is the energy of Pisces, a subtle force that feels its way in life. The energy uses the power of intuition to make decisions while not necessarily set to one course. Known for creativity and a vast imagination, they see beyond what others may not and move forward with an inner self-belief. Touched by the divine, they sense more than what meets the eye, beyond the physical realm. However, with such heightened sensitivity, the Pisces can lack grounding and can become too out of touch with reality.

When Pisces is at the cusp of the 10th house, it can feel like nothing holds, nothing stays grounded in the vast limitless space it naturally experiences. The traditional structures of the 10th house tend to be non-sensical, dismantled slowly and the more force or effort used turns frustrating and confusing. There is a sense of displacement in the experience of goals and career. Without awareness, and as they see others set career goals, defining steps to achieve and reach success and satisfaction, the native with Pisces at the cusp of their 10th house feels disillusioned about the world and their place in it.

Navigating your 10th House Like an Aerospace Engineer

Before going up to space and geared up in 300-pound equipment, astronauts must go through intensive underwater training in an anti-gravitational pool. With the assistance of four to six highly skilled divers who watch over them, the simulation prepares their work in a zero-gravity environment by floating in the water while performing a complicated task. Pisces in the 10th house requires the native to be suited up in intuition, to allow the ebbs and flow to guide you in your goals, legacy and career. If in doubt, there is divine guidance, angels that keep an eye on you and your process, giving you nudges, signs and lessons to keep you aligned, to help you let go and trust.

![](/images/uploads/2023/03/3-640x640.png)Being Effective By Letting Go

Characteristically unifying and boundless, Pisces at the cusp indicates that ego, material pursuits and attachment to status and recognition must be surrendered. Though possible to achieve, it is not the focus, rather the idea is aiming for meaning and purpose, to heal or be of service to others. Goals are set to heighten spirituality and intuition and what you build on and hone to maintain status is the structures and expression of imagination and compassion.

Seen as an egoless area, the job can also be viewed as an experience, Pisces style. A kind of place where miracles and beauty reside, the ideal experience expressed by the divine student develops and exemplifies non-linear modes. Through intuitive guidance and action and the wisdom of perception and belief, the native understands the bounds beyond traditional structures. The qualities of creativity and imagination are at the forefront, a divine mapping in the making, charting an experience worthy of the heavens on earth.

Generating Income

Though connected to 10th house functions, financial resources are determined by your 2nd house qualities and determined by the weight of currency in confidence and self-worth to create and attract wealth.  When considering whole houses, your Ascendent would be in Gemini, thus having one or more income streams is beneficial. With Cancer at the cusp colouring your 2nd house, take initiative and reinforce intuition to build wealth. In essence, allow and trust money to flow in and out.

_Transcend goals, status and rewards of material wealth and success beyond the material world_

Expression for Virgo and Pisces axis.

Maintaining confidence, creative structures and consistency in your craft are achieved by applying the opposite and grounded sign of Virgo. Consider the phrase in your life: Practice makes perfect. Virgo is “the grind” sign, doing the job in itself is satisfactory. They cherish the moment by embracing the task at hand, seeking to improve themselves all through life. Virgo qualities are the tethers that help focus on what matters, on striving to get better.

When on a spacewalk and to do the job safely, astronauts are limited in time when performing tasks.  A person with a career expressed in the sign of Pisces will not always be a consistent run. The ups and downs—the natural flow that occurs should be viewed as downtime to reinforce and improve inspiration, craft and purpose. Let your imagination roam, and nourish intuition between your obligation to work and your career.

Saturn in the Chart and Building Confidence to Trust Oneself to Flow

Initially, especially in youth, right up to your 40’s—at least until after Saturn’s Return, feelings of uncertainty and confusion about your career path are likely. Along with purpose and meaning, look to Saturn and the sign to understand qualities and states of inadequacy and limitation. Functions of the house indicate where you are meant to master a skill, hone and improve. It can also indicate(along with other things) career prospects.

Confidence can be gained where Saturn sits in a personal chart. The sign and house also show areas of focus and potential satisfaction. With the prescribed detachment, the sign helps you achieve your 10th house dreams, and goals into achievements. Backing yourself with skills, proficiency and expertise in the craft will help build the confidence and chops to trust and move into the ebb and flow. Working with the qualities of natal Saturn can help you build a reputation on your own terms.
