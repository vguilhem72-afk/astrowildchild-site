This Virgo Full Moon may be thrown off by a surprising splash from the Uranus in Taurus connection. Full Moon’s remind us about the necessary ebb and flow of life, that sometimes letting go opens up new possibilities. A period when we can also gain useful insight or a reach a culmination into matters relative to the area of life it transits. The link it makes to the radical energy of Uranus can at first feel like an added disruption to the normally calm Virgo waters and trigger its more OCD side, influencing our own emotional flow. To avoid the undertow, ride the tides of discernment, flexibility and the strategic nature the Virgo energy is known for. In this way, you’ll notice the incoming waves of exciting, long-lasting and valuable opportunities coming through. Swimming with the Taurus Uranian currents should feel refreshing.

Happy Full Moon in Virgo!
