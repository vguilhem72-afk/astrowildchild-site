The Full Moon shines its light in the pensive sign of Aquarius.

As with the Moon’s final phase of illumination, there’s culminations involved, clarity to be gained and endings reached. An energy that we normally sense days prior the phenomenon. This Full Moon, however, does not come without tension, which can turn emotions detached and cold. The conflict comes from revolutionary Uranus in Taurus, dropping uninvited news, causing abrupt changes that can create splintering between two different areas of life—varying in severity, depending on personal chart.

The positive is that there is synergy between the two seemingly squaring forces. Riding on the higher frequency may not be that difficult of a jump. With the great awakener Uranus, involved, no doubt, the shock to the system is a wake-up call. One that usually points to necessary reformation and long overdo authentication.

The best way forward is by looking through an objective lens, seeing the reality of the matter, without discounting emotional needs. It’s a balance perspective that will help us see the value to all parts of the equation. It’s a wider view showing the best direction for progressive changes, one that carves out a more signature path.

Happy Aquarius Full Moon!
