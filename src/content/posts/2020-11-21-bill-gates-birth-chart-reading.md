## A Deep Dive Into Bill Gates Birth Chart

_**![Bill Gates Birth Chart Wheel](/images/uploads/2020/11/BillGatesChart-640x459.png)From Tech King to philanthropist, Bill Gates**_ has ruled both worlds with his innovative ideas—developing the user-friendly interface Microsoft Windows, and his generous contributions through a charity he created with his wife, called the Melinda and Bill Gates foundation. A cause that focuses on health, education and poverty. He was named the most generous philanthropist in 2018, donating billions of his money into the charity. Despite expressing his reason for his mammoth contribution as meaningful work, with his past ruthless behaviour and tactics following him, his altruism has not convinced everyone. [https://tinyurl.com/roqw5sw](https://tinyurl.com/roqw5sw)

Aside from the fact that donating to charity gives a company tax incentives, the foundation he created and sits as one of three trustees continues to generate billions more in his pocket. In fact, the foundation has given him more power and clout than he had before. He has built another empire, this time a reach extending to different sectors of life, from health, education to climate change. According to Rob Larson from Jacobin, his move into charitable causes conveniently came at a time when his power moves, as a savage IT giant, started to catch up to him.

[“Twenty years ago, people associated the name Gates with ‘ruthless, predatory’ monopolistic conduct.” However, “after taking a public relations beating during \[the Microsoft antitrust\] trial’s early going in late 1998, the company started what was described at the time as a ‘charm offensive’ aimed at improving its image . . . Mr. Gates contributed $20.3 billion, or 71 percent of his total contributions to the foundation . . . during the 18 months between the start of the trial and the verdict.” A wealth manager frankly states, “his philanthropy has helped ‘rebrand’ his name.”](https://www.jacobinmag.com/2020/04/bill-gates-foundation-philanthropy-microsoft) 

The question remains, what’s really in it for him? Are his motives truly altruistic or a clever move to salvage his name, another funnel to feed his voracious appetite for power?

To begin all of his angular houses are activated by cardinal signs, the main ingredients of a personality that can create a leader. To stabilize the action oriented energy are his fixed planets in the signs of [Leo](https://astrowildchild.com/star-signs/leo-self-mastery/) and [Scorpio](https://astrowildchild.com/star-signs/scorpio-the-super-healer/). An intense and stubborn quality he possess and with the exception of his North Node in Sagittarius, it is not surprising the man has no mutable energy to show a level of compromise in his personality.

### **The Evolution of His Personality**

Gates was well known for bullying his employees during his Microsoft days. He was known to be cruel, predatory and condescending. He infamously harassed his staff with endless questions. A kind of ritual for the microchip tycoon to eventually prove their incompetency. He was also known to circle around the company parking lot after hours to see who was putting in the time.

A stickler for results his bullying extended to business associates and competitors.

 [“…it was Gates’s burning desire to grind his competitors to sand, along with his desire to set the standard that software makers would conform to, that led to Microsoft selling over 90 percent of the PC operating systems of the 1990s and 2000s. That dominance made Gates the world’s richest man for decades on end.”](https://www.jacobinmag.com/2020/04/bill-gates-foundation-philanthropy-microsoft)

The cuddle-loving sign of [Cancer](https://astrowildchild.com/star-signs/cancer-masters-of-emotions/) on his rising would have you think otherwise, but keep in mind, Cancer energy is of cardinal modality, one that pushes for what it wants. The entrepreneurial force is strong in him (Cancer +[Aries](https://astrowildchild.com/star-signs/cancer-masters-of-emotions/)). His moon is in the sign of Aries right at the top of his chart. (His emotions splattered in the media for all to see) A Moon in Aries gives him compulsive, reactive emotions. A moon he shares with the late Steve Jobs—also known for berating his staff, it’s considered one of the selfish, more infantile moons, in demand for what it wants. However, this is the emotional energy that pushes for individual vision, that isn’t waiting for approval and isn’t afraid to go for what it wants. With an opposition to his [Mars](https://astrowildchild.com/astrology-elements/mars-our-fighting-spirit/) in [Libra](https://astrowildchild.com/astrology-elements/libra-mastering-relationships/), in the 4th house, it also indicates a short fuse, temperamental and projecting inner turmoil onto others.  A confrontational moon, that signifies fault blaming in others, not seeing their own hands in it. His impatience and aggression are further heightened by another opposition to Mercury, also in logical and pushy Libra. Gates is pushy with words and can spitball them in anger.
