Energy will soar as we bust out from Pisces dreamy waters and into the assertive and firey sign of Aries. As the baby of the astros, we may feel a new sense of self, a refreshing break from the passive and unifying Pisces energy.

If we’ve done the work and set our intentions from the last New Moon in Pisces then it’s harvest time and the Full Super Moon in Libra will feel like premium grade support, fuelling the Aries steam.

The tides will turn again to a dynamic, excitable new moon, as it enters Aries, lubing our confidence and perhaps our dampened independence squeaking from all the emotional waters we’ve been treading in.

Make use of the remaining week and a half with Mercury retro in Pisces to do a final intuitive fine tuning, which will keep us flexible once Saturn in Capricorn makes a square to the Sun in Aries. He will act as road signs, the speed limit, giving focus and restrain to the high level, all-action oriented pace we may be in.

Frustrations may continue to rise with a road block ahead by a discordant square to Pluto in Capricorn. It may seem like a crises with impatience at a high and an amped up energy in the air. It’s always wise to surrender to the no-shit taken energy of Pluto. It will be a choice between being served a ticket or getting a fair warning.

The following energy of Mercury turning direct in Pisces tells you to step back, put that gear in neutral for a moment and assess. A power struggle between what you want and what is expected won’t last that long, it’s a Sun transit after all. In the mean time, know that help is along the way as Venus will change into the familiar waters of Pisces, having the ability to charm Pluto with a sextile the very next day. In other words, stay adaptable, use grace and ride on intuition. It may mean taking a detour, slowing down, but it also means getting to smell the flowers and enjoying the scenery.
