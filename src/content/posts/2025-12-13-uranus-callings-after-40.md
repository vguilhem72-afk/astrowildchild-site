## **![Uranus Callings: So Much to Look Forward to After 40](/images/uploads/2025/12/ydaWPPEjv7MfcyuR0VRw-3-8j5ly-640x640.jpg)**Uranus Callings: How They Help You look Forward As You Age

They say that when you start to get older, you are looking back more than you are forward.

Whoever “they” are, obviously weren’t in their Uranian energy.

**Whenever [Uranus](https://astrowildchild.com/uranus-in-astrology/) is in focus, Saturn is somehow related.**

After your Saturn return (between 28–35—its full effect), the next major transit of adulthood is transit Saturn square your natal Uranus around ages 36–42.

This is the first nudge toward breaking from Saturn’s conventions. What you’ve been conditioned to—whether through family, culture, or societal norms—the sense of obligation and expectation begins clashing with your Uranian authenticity. This period asks for constant adjustment, trying to appease both parts of your life.

If you don’t already have strong Uranian energy, this first nudge can feel like an inconvenient awakening. Though subtle, it never quite leaves; instead, it gradually builds, waning at times and becoming more apparent at others. It comes from the pressure of Saturn poking the bear.

Saturn’s pestering is actually waking this part of you into awareness—inviting you to consider whether you still want to follow the father figure’s conventions. It has you questioning responsibilities to date and whether you want to continue on that road. You may even start asking if this is all life has to offer. It’s subtly annoying, and Saturn has a way of stirring fear to push you toward action. (square)

On the other side, Uranus has you questioning Saturn’s limitations and authority. It’s a period of constant inner struggle that demands new ways of doing things. Yeah, it’s uncomfortable, and you’re likely to avoid the whispers of change. But this discomfort marks the starting point of growth into something more authentic.

Most people stay with the traditional and familiar (Saturn). But the same tension returns later when Uranus opposes your natal Uranus—the mid-life crisis that wasn’t sudden at all, but simply becomes harder to ignore than the earlier Saturn–Uranus square. More on this later.

### There are other major planets that encourage this change.

**Pluto square natal Pluto** **(between 36–42, but varying by birthdate since Pluto lingers in signs longer than other planets)**

This acts as attempted support (although it rarely feels supportive), or more precisely, as pressure to eliminate whatever enslaves or disempowers you from the past. The purpose of this extended period is destruction—a kind of forest-fire phenomenon that eventually allows new growth.

**2nd Nodal Return (age 36/37)**

When your lunar nodes return to the sign of your birth. A cleansing and a check-in with your spiritual mission.

So, over the two years when Saturn and Uranus are square, you’re pushed toward either more Saturn or more Uranus. Neither path is wrong. It’s simply about choosing between inviting fresh, Uranian energy or sticking with the familiar pattern (perhaps with modest growth), which still leads along a Saturnian trajectory. And yes, that includes feeling, thinking, and seeing life through a more traditional Saturn lens—looking back, fearing aging, and viewing youth as bygone, generally speaking.

However, if you’re bold enough to reset, reevaluate your priorities, and welcome fresh ideas and new energy—if you’re not being guided by fear—there is life after middle age. Not the façade of complacent contentment, but truly living with self-acceptance and embracing where you are, grounded in gratitude for all that you’ve learned and experienced—ironically gained through Saturn’s conditioning.

Those sparks of insight, sudden ideas, and breakthrough moments that arise before transit Uranus opposes your natal Uranus—[Jeffrey Wolf Green notes in _Uranus: Freedom From the Known_](https://www.amazon.ca/Uranus-Freedom-Jeffrey-Wolf-Green/dp/1533574154/ref=sr_1_1?crid=BRBLW3N15XVH&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.jNC0SCoAprjY9ZTH_FkIVhqVo3wtMFeWOgisaMNeqPJltd36HIn7dkODDhSSd9UfIyo_d8hooPDY1l4BCMOcIlUJuBb6hBSnUNj5jFAyI3c.Nc3MVG2TKL3v0AcAraZEVY-F-_z2hUcJpT8tOv6Snp0&dib_tag=se&keywords=jeffrey+wolf+green+freedom+of+the+known&qid=1765582275&sprefix=jeffrey+wolf+green+freedom+of+the+known%2Caps%2C375&sr=8-1)—are the call of Uranian growth. The unknown calling with the message that there is still much to look forward to. That you are still growing, with new paths to pave. And that path is created by your newer self, more assured, shaped by years of expectation, yet no longer needing to fully obey them, because you’re beginning to create your own way.

The square period is training you beyond Saturn’s ways of being, connecting you to something higher—your higher self—and to the collective consciousness.

#### **Beyond Saturn**

Saturn symbolizes fitting in and cooperating with rules, which remains relevant if you don’t want to clash with Saturn’s laws—but now, those rules can be bent. You’ve learned to find loopholes that let you live in ways that feel true to you.

It’s akin to mastering the rules of writing, then breaking them with confidence.

Uranus pushes you to peel away from Saturn’s obligations (the thickness of the layers varies). Uranus transits open opportunities to do things you never imagined—things that once felt ridiculous, paradoxical, frightening, yet exciting and deeply aligned with your authenticity.

Uranus trains you to live more in line with your self-truth—a natural progression.

As ruler of the 11th, opposite Leo’s 5th House of creative identity (which seeks approval and an audience), Uranus shifts you toward offering your “specialness” to society and the interconnectedness of life. It’s your chance to express individuality as authentic contribution.

Uranus is the welcome mat to “getting to know” the outer planetary energies and embodying their connection. It is the gateway from Saturn’s reality to the outer planetary collective consciousness.

-   **Neptune’s spiritual connection and embrace.**
-   **Pluto’s subconscious healing and truth.**

If you’ve ever observed any truly successful person—whether celebrated publicly or quietly walking their path—you’ll find that fulfillment comes from being of service, connecting, and contributing to humanity. (A mission centered only on the self is limiting and devoid of growth.) And if you consciously work with Uranus, you gain the opportunity to offer your unique abilities to the world.

Uranus—your inner Uranus—opens a whole new world defined by you, shaped by your imagined possibilities, ready for exploration.

When you’re doing what you’re meant to be doing, able to express who you truly are, well, of course there’s something to look forward to.
