## Jupiter in Astrology: Expansion, Wisdom and Hope

![jupiter in astrology](/images/uploads/2020/12/1.png)Whenever I interact with someone, whether or not I’ve had the privilege to read their chart or been granted entrance beyond the interface of their Rising sign or [1st House](https://astrowildchild.com/astrology-elements/1st-house-in-astrology/), a matrix of possibilities for their behaviour appears in my mind. Both intuition and logic start to work out the complex threads of their personality. My astrologer mind is inclined to connect their personal planets to the appropriate constellation. I know their natal Jupiter has come into orbit once opinions are expressed and convictions are shared, knitting a broader scope of character by code of conduct, mores, and principles.

Jupiter is the first impersonal outer planet from the Earth’s orbit. The planet ruling the higher mind is our social connection to the outside world through our [Mars](https://astrowildchild.com/astrology-elements/mars-in-astrology/) action and the experiences we accumulate. Made up of our Moon’s nurturing past and Venus’s choices, we assimilate Mercury’s understanding of the consequences of our actions into our grand beliefs. What we deem socially appropriate, Jupiter is our reference manual to guide us as we navigate the world. Since Jupiter takes about 12 years to circle the sun, it generally stays in a sign for a year. An intergenerational quality of energy is shared with those born within a span of a year.

In the fire signs, principles can be based on creative identity and will. An energy that pushes its beliefs through inspiring displays. An airy Jupiter will assimilate beliefs about what the mind sees, a logical and rational system of principles, it can be detached in its views. It’s an all-seeing eye that believes in collecting and disseminating information. 

Jupiter in water element is the higher mind working with instinct and emotional intelligence, feeling out personal truths and principles. In an earth sign, the higher view is brought down to reality. An earthy Jupiter assimilates through empirical observation, mores formed from facts and practical experiences.

It’s also known as the planet that expands, that gives luck and opportunity in at least one area of life. In the cardinal Houses, it can expand the belief in self and security and provide ample luck in familial and relationship settings and career opportunities. The succedent houses expand material resources, creative talent, luck in business ventures, and showers benefits in social circles. While the cadent sector tends to expand interaction with others, favours work, one’s health or the higher mind, opportunity and luck come from faith in the process of life.

Where there is expansion, there is also a potential for overindulgence, abuse, greed, and immoral behaviour. It’s important to be cautious and aware of this potential when under the influence of Jupiter.

In the earth element, it can get stuck, narrowed in its belief, slowing growth, and overindulging in material desires. A watery Jupiter can get carried away with emotions, drown in its depths and get lost in irrational seas. An air sign can be given as a gift for gab but can take advantage of information and ideas and turn them into slander and gossip. While the fiery signs can become egotistical and overly dramatic, arrogance can cloud its vision and become self-serving.

Only through life experiences and insight do we fully benefit from Jupiter’s energy.  Also associated with wisdom and freedom, we don’t fully come into our Jupiter energy until the ages of 55 to 67, according to ancient astrologer Ptolemy’s 7 stages of life. A period in life where we have gained inner and outer wealth and accumulated enough awareness to teach and share. Generally, it is a time when we enjoy abundant material resources and wisdom. A set-up to live our best lives, to find what liberty genuinely means to us. These are the golden years of our lives before we enter Saturn’s slower, more reflective, mature age.
