## The 1st House in Astrology: Input and Output and how we Experience the World

![The 1st House in Astrology](/images/uploads/2020/04/3-640x580.png)

You probably shouldn’t judge a book by its cover. But what about how it wants to be seen?

Immediate impressions are the stuff of the 1st House. With all things equal, it is the skin we wear when we first meet people, what we project, and how we size up a person.

“The first impression is always the right one. I rarely change my mind upward about people.”

A quote from gonzo writer Hunter S. Thompson and spoken like an upfront [Aries](https://astrowildchild.com/star-signs/aries-in-astrology/) ascendent. He says it like how it is, expecting others to be the same. “As it is both the maze we create and the way out,” according to Howard Sasportas. “It points to what we are looking for and what is doing the looking.”

The moment we took breath, whatever sign was at the easternmost point of the horizon is our rising sign and degree, which informs the function of our 1st House and the beginning pages chart’s storyline. The energy of that sign is a layer of dermis, acting as the avatar we wear and the interface that maps out our personal and exterior reality.

Similar to signs, each House functions in modalities. The first House is an angular house, analogous to cardinal qualities, and it is the threshold for the rest of the natal chart. This is where we discover our own unique lens. The hue coated by the sign at our rising or cusp can tell us how we want to experience and tackle life.

A mutable Gemini Rising can flow fluidly, communicating and exchanging ideas with others. Curious by default, they experience life through interactions, socializing and assimilating information. Their social nature caters to the versatility and nuance they seek, initiating ideas through everyday exchanges.  A fixed and detached Aquarius will likely see and do things differently in their own systematic way, seeing beyond what is expected and required. An earthy Taurus takes on a slower, steady pace, experiencing the world through the five senses, physical pleasures, forms of art, particularly music.

As the interface to the world, the first House also embodies our physical appearance, vitality and how well we maintain the wear and tear. Virgo’s concern with physical health will continually seek to improve vitality. Their humble and reticent nature will show in their fashion sense. While a Leo Ascendant may have a more expressive and vibrant wardrobe, they tend to look for physical, sporty activities to maintain health.

What you see isn’t always what you get.  Just as complicated as human beings are—having parts to us that are only privy to a few, while considering personal values, cultural conditionings and keeping in mind our continual evolution—so is the chart. Our first House is also our front-of-the-line soldier, filtering and screening what is tolerated and unacceptable before granting entrance into the personal parts of our lives.

![](/images/uploads/2020/05/hunter-s-mark-zeilman.jpg)I recently had a conversation with a woman who had commented on a Pisces story I posted. She shared the strong currents of her Pisces chart and that she was also an Aries Rising. I asked if she loved having the protective gear of Aries on her Ascendant. To which she answered— “I’m so grateful for it. I don’t let anyone fu\*k with me.”

Our first House can be used as a coping mechanism to deal with the outside world. The fortress we build from the bricks of our Rising sign energy. A Gemini uses its silver tongue or can deflect conversation to something lighter and favourable, while a Scorpio armour can also have the boldness of an Aries, they’re more likely to deposit a stressful situation in their mental archive for examination and later use.

For others, the powerhouse of the 1st House truly represents the strength and stability of the chart.

Having a rising sign that rides on frenetic currents and prefers to cut to the chase, Thompson crossed out anyone who didn’t show their true selves right out of the gate. He has Saturn conjunct his first House, while Jupiter is in Capricorn. Impenetrable, his beliefs are the stronghold of his first House.

Also, having his ruling planet Mars in Scorpio and innate qualities of his Moon in Sagittarius in the 8th House affirms the raw and deep truth he seeks from others. When we look at the base of his chart, he has his Sun in Cancer in the fourth, the truth is both of comfort and the foundation of his spirit.

The 1st House is the door from which we meet others. The sign at the cusp of our Ascendant is our façade style, and the appearance can tell us if we upkeep or not. The immediate impressions between one-on-one contact, our 1st House is our social avant-garde, evaluating from and dispatching qualities of our rising sign. It takes the helm of our chart, leading and informing other elements of our personality and reality— forming our values, adding a shade to our Mercury way of thinking and paving the path to our Sun soul sign development. 

“It is the quest of our Ascendant on which we must embark to find the kind of hero our Sun sign was born to be.” [Liz Greene](https://www.amazon.com/Liz-Greene/e/B000APUE0G%3Fref=dbs_a_mng_rwt_scns_share)
