There’s a cloudy layer veiling the Full Super Moon in Virgo. Just how thick depends on the individual chart.

An elusive, enchanting influence by Neptune in Pisces, the normally straining opposition between our ego & emotion, inner world & outer, our wants & needs can get confusing & deceptive at this time. Nothing is what it seems. Whatever it is that we are shown, whatever it is that seems clear, may not be. Since it involves the Sun, the ego may hold onto, feed on what it can to kill doubt, to satiate or distract. An effect that can scatter emotion, thoughts & misalign boundaries.

To help wade through the haze, turn the lower energies of the opposition into the higher frequencies of Neptune in Pisces. Get creative, meditate, connect though compassion, bridge the oppositions in unification.

The smoke can be further cleared by tapping into other areas of life in grounding Capricorn energy. Mars can give constructive & practical insight in action. Jupiter, optimism & belief in the mundane. Pluto gives trust to intense & strong emotion based on reality.

Ideally, if we can hold off from making any concrete decisions or moving forward in decisive action until Mercury, Virgo’s ruling planet, goes in direct motion on the 11th, more clarity can be gained.

The Pisces & Virgo axis is about self-less service, both spiritual in nature, yet operating from different planes of perception. One requires the power of intuition, while the other relies on power of practical discernment to create boundaries. If moving forward is a must make sure one follows the other.
