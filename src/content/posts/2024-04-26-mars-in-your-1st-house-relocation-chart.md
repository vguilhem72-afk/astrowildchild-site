# ![Mars in the 1st House Relocation Chart](/images/uploads/2024/04/OracleCardNftImage-640x645.jpg)How Mars in your 1st House Relocation Chart Benefits You

If you’re the type of person that often…

-   has issues with setting healthy boundaries
-   turn into a Venus-oriented people-pleaser
-   depends on others to feel whole
-   requires validation and approval from others
-   has difficulties making decisions

…having your Mars in the [1st House](https://astrowildchild.com/the-1st-house-in-astrology/) is star-sent.

Personally, I don’t like disappointing other people. Sometimes, I fuss over it so much that I find ways to overcompensate. At times in my life, it became an issue on my well-being. I have Venus square Jupiter. This is fantastic for other reasons, but it can inflate my Venus inclination to cater to others at the sacrifice of my needs.

I thought this was normal for a good period of my life. That is until resentment started to hit. It’s a see-sawing effect with the aspect that alternated to both extremes- withdrawing entirely at times and then finding myself back to unhealthy giving. But when I found ways to rebalance or mitigate my detrimental tendency, I could do more things for myself and devote my time to those who really matter.

_Mars in your 1st House Relocation Chart can help tremendously._ 

**What is a Relocation Chart?**

A Relocation Chart is a recalculated natal chart set in a different locale in the world, away from where one was born but in the same moment in time with the same snapshot of the sky.  What changes are your Rising Sign,  the sign at each house cusps and planet house position. The planet signs and degrees stay the same, as found in your natal chart. To read further [go here.](https://astrowildchild.com/a-relocation-chart-in-astrology/)

(With all relocation energy and depending on one’s level of sensitivity, the length of time the planets are felt varies, but it is common to feel your first House energy sooner than the other houses)

**What is Mars?**

Mars is our action-oriented selves. What we tend to focus on and be driven towards. It is our desire nature and can tell us what invigorates and what can deplete us. It is a naturally selfish energy that joys in severing the unnecessaries. In the first House, it is a self-possessed energy. It’s inclined to take care of oneself first and foremost, including physical health and how one wants to experience life.

You can become self-sufficient or learn to develop it over time if you relocate to a Mars in the 1st House environment. If you want to be fierce, bold and not give a shit about what others think, a Mars relocated in your 1st House is a good way to practice this. This is an energy that helps you go after what you want decisively.

**What is the 1st House?**

In a nutshell, it is your first point of contact with the world, and the sign it is in tells you how you will go about experiencing and getting what you want. It can also tell you if you’re inclined to be extroverted or introverted. It also indicates your physical attributes and your style of fashion. The [1st House](https://astrowildchild.com/the-1st-house-in-astrology/) is the window into your interior self and informs the rest of your houses (areas of life). The 1st house is your modus operandi in how you interact with others and the navigational starting point to how you experience the world.

The combination of Mars in the 1st House is strong and makes you act and go for what you want, often blazing through without considering the consequences or assessing what’s around. This can be liberating for those overwhelmed by analysis paralysis or if their general Mars energy is stifled.

There are always downfalls, and the general health of your chart will indicate your personal Mars expression. The pitfalls of Mars in the 1st House:

-   extreme selfishness
-   overaggression
-   quick to anger
-   unadaptable
-   confrontational
-   tunnel vision
-   impulsive

The sign at the cusp and the sign that Mars falls in determines or shapes the combination into better definition. If it is debilitated or in bad shape with challenging aspects, Mars’ discomforts will be projected out and will require a work-through.

## Mars in Fire Signs

Fire signs are meant to shine. Like the element, there is a glow; others cannot help but look. As general extroverts, all of the self-assertive, social signs feel comfortable in the 1st house of self that is conducive to their elemental characteristics. The qualities of the first house keep the fire signs burning physically and spiritually.

Aries Mars in your 1st House 

Arguably, the best combo with Mars is in its home sign and house it rules, it is in optimal comfort. You embody the Aries “I am” motto.

You will feel vigour, drive and motivation to get things done. You will focus on maintaining physical health, adding to an already regenerative energy. This placement blazes through life without needing others’ acceptance, approval or help. If you’re in a pioneering spirit, a relocation chart with Mars in your 1st house will boost your confidence to curate your goals into manifestation. Your impulse is through the roof, giving you a laser-like, can-do attitude.

However, note that this is a sign that can lead to extreme cases of selfishness, a single-minded intensity that can turn into inconsiderate and aggressive behaviour.

Leo Mars in the 1st House

A great combination for soul-level creative expression, Leo energy likes to flare in its identity. Its natural desire to shine fits well in the 1st house platform of self and being. People will be drawn to their warm and inspirational light.

If this is you by relocation, your confidence radiates to help others grow. You can feel bolder to strut who you are without worrying about what others think. Having your Mars in the 1st House by relocation is a positive way to understand your creative identity, feeling your own light by directly experiencing through interaction and feedback from others. Leo in Mars can help you develop self-pride and enthusiasm. It can also help you build and hone your infectious and generous characteristics.

The thing that can burn out the energy is extreme pride, egotism, and selfishly taking the spotlight. Not being aware of your surroundings can cause you to arrogantly dim other people’s light.

Sagittarius Mars in the 1st House

The Archer is the most mature of the fire signs. The highest expression is walking in your wisdom, inspiring and motivating others to find their own truth. It’s a social sign and has passed the consideration of Libra and Scorpio. Sagittarius Mars in the 1st House has a wider lens of experience, and the energy can move in symbiosis. It likes to have people around to communicate and bounce ideas. It’s a very generous sign and burns brighter when it sees or knows that others around them are also in good spirits. The more, the merrier is a perfect idiom for the Sagittarius Mars.

By embodying its wider lens, if you have Mars in Sagittarius by relocation, you can start to understand yourself as separate from others. Your truth vs. theirs. On a micro/ personal level, the position in the 1st house can aid you in practicing healthy boundaries. You can embody the wisdom of personal space and choices, which allows you to be free of other people’s expectations. It’s a position that amplifies your own truth and philosophy, giving light to different ways of being.

As long as this combo is working under awareness, you should be able to avoid the pitfalls of fiery Mars in the 1st house. All should be good if the wisdom gained involves the acceptance of self and beliefs without arrogance.

### Mars in Earth Signs

Earth signs Taurus, Virgo and Capricorn are stable energies. They are contained, goal-oriented, determined–signs that look for tangible results. Mars in Earth signs is strategic, thinks before it acts and understands how to make things happen. This could be through trial and error and eventually learning and achieving efficiency. It’s about practice making perfect, being grounded and embodying your physical senses.

An Earth Mars in the 1st House is about self-reliance, self-involvement and self-improvement. A one-track Mars that looks to reach results, this Mars will stop at nothing to get what it wants, even if it takes a lifetime to reach. (usually, it won’t with an efficient and strategized type Mars)

![Mars in Taurus in your 1st House Relocation Chart](/images/uploads/2024/04/use4website-640x798.jpg)Mars in Taurus in the 1st House

Mars in Taurus is the slow-but-steady wins-the-race-energy. It feels things out through its zen-like warrior spirit. Mars in Taurus reminds me of a Shaolin Kung Fu Master in touch with their five senses and manipulating their flow. Like the characters of Flying Tiger Hidden Dragon, your Mars in Taurus physical self has a relationship to its surroundings and can anticipate moves like a ninja.

When you have your Mars in the 1st House by relocation, you can be more in flow with the five senses directly. You are peaceful by nature. Thus, you only move when provoked or stimulated. You can develop experiences and interactions with others with more energy and force, yet with focus and patience. The energetic combination can help you stay present and enjoy what your earthly senses offer.

Its rage is in the form of a bull and acts on provocation. Regulate how you act on things and practice a balance of assertion and anger before turning into a bull warrior who sees red.

Mars in Virgo in the 1st House

Virgo Mars is unassuming, adaptable and would rather avoid conflict, which usually means taking flight rather than confronting it head-on. It would rather yield than rock the boat. Poised when confronted, a Virgo Mars will find logical solutions to get results and won’t be satisfied until one is found.

Mars in Virgo aims to get things done without the frills. It’s focus-bound. Mars in Virgo in the 1st house can help optimize your health. You can develop more organization and a general routine with this energy. Virgo is the sign of balance and is associated with the number 6 of service. In the first house, it turns into self-service, which can benefit you if you’ve habitually focused on others. You can turn recluse, similar to the Hermit Tarot card meaning, helping you devote energy and drive into a long, grinding project.

I would watch out for extreme perfectionism in how you appear. How you see yourself through others’ eyes. You can become self-critical and project that onto others. Awkward and resistant to allow connections in— for fear of being judged and criticized. It can turn into a debilitating position.

Mars in Capricorn in the 1st House

Mars in Capricorn is exalted. In other words, Mars’ natural attributes are accentuated and expressed well in the ambitious sign of Capricorn, making this a highly goal-oriented combination. Like Virgo, they will stop at nothing to achieve goals. Mars in Capricorn also looks to be the G.O.AT. The mountain goat Mars reaches peaks and strives to maintain that high status. Their drive to succeed is second to none.

By relocation chart, you can develop strategy, become more efficient and learn/embody how to execute plans to the finish line—especially if you’re looking to self-improve. How you operate, see things and want to maneuver in Mars Capricorn style will help you become the best version of yourself and will trickle down to all the other houses. You might find enjoyment in improving yourself because you are perceived and received in a good light. This gives you thoughtful and deliberate action, highlighting power moves in how you interact and navigate life.

Like Virgo, this combination can be quite critical and have high self-expectations. Which is healthy until you project it onto others through harsh criticism, condescending judgment and cold detachment. A good balance to this position is to learn to be easy on yourself.

#### Mars in Air Signs 

Air signs Gemini, Libra, and Aquarius are social signs. They rule the third house of siblings, early socialization of the third, 7th house partnerships and 11th house friends and community. They love and quite need connections from others to share ideas and bounce thoughts and concepts.

Communication is important to Mars in Air signs.  Because Mars is our physical self, it is an energy that is highly energized by social settings. They move and are moved by interaction. Mars here has a desire to create connections and focus on people and community. It expresses itself intellectually, logically and in a quick-witted manner.

Mars in Gemini in the 1st House

Mars in Gemini is motivated by variety. If you have this combination, you are an influx of energy. Gemini is frenetic and wants to move continuously. You will move with speed but not with caution. You will love to delve into information, research, and gather as much knowledge and technology as possible.

This is great for flexibility and can make you more social, especially if you have dominating fixed signs in your chart or are intimidated by meeting others and being in new social settings. Initially, you may feel overwhelmed and anxious with Gemini ruling the nervous system. But as a Gemini, you are systematic and resourceful; connecting with others is vital to you. You will find a way to overcome the hyper, destabilizing energy you initially feel.

Discovery is upon you! You’ll find yourself more charming and relaxed when you learn to bounce ideas with others. The 1st House Gemini is a learning-for-life sign; the more you know, the better you become with people. The nature of the 1st House will enable you to understand your personal desires and how you want to project them onto others.

Mars in Gemini in the 1st house can mitigate that selfish 1st House, self-involvement energy because of Gemini’s nature to want to connect, which requires sharing and consideration. The only thing I would look at for is the need for too much variety and never getting anything accomplished or completed. This is not a very grounded combination and can feel dizzying and superficial, perpetuating that selfish behaviour because of the lack of solid connections found. It’s also restless energy and focus will need a bit of finesse.

Mars in Libra in the 1st House

Mars is weak in the relationship sign of Libra. It is action-oriented and wants to move when it does. Libra, its exact opposite sign, likes to weigh things out. Its propensity to step back and look at both sides impedes Mars’ natural sense of forceful, speedy drive.

In the first house, you could feel like the brakes are always on. You can easily become frustrated, lose momentum or plagued by indecision. The best case scenario is to use the best of each energy and find a middle ground. Take the Mars direct force and self-confidence and back it up by Libra thought and consideration.

This is good for developing diplomatic relationships if you find it hard to negotiate, reciprocate, or find a good balance between your needs and others. Libra Mars in the first house is a chance to understand your own boundaries and to find a happy medium with others. It can teach you the positives of putting yourself first so that you can learn healthy sharing and compromise.

Mars in Aquarius in the 1st House

Mars in Aquarius is a combination that acts on confident self-assurance. Aquarius is knowledge-oriented, deliberate and devoted to its vision. It is ruled by Saturn, the structure energy that plans and assesses into achievable outcomes. Mars in Aquarius will work on what they want without the approval of others. Similar to Aries, it does not need the approval of others and will work steadfastly and ambitiously on their ideals and ideas to completion.

Mars in Aquarius in the 1st house can help you focus and achieve your extraordinary visions. If there is uncertainty, clarity can be gained and the process initiated with Mars in the 1st house. Nothing will stop you from making a dream or a vision a reality. It is an ideal placement to assert your authenticity and quirkiness.

The one thing I would look out for with Mars in Aquarius in your 1st house is that your actions, focus and determination can isolate others. You are the visionary, and once you’re comfortable with your own authenticity, you can be the light that reflects that onto others to help them express their own individuality. On low vibration, when rebellion turns into a superior complex, you detach yourself from the fabric of life rather than pouring your unique contribution into it.

**Mars in the Water Signs**

In the watery signs of Cancer, Pisces and Scorpio, Mars is the spiritual warrior. An energy that often takes time to develop before it feels comfortable in its fluid position. Mars is about speed, a sharpshooter, and is action-oriented, usually slowed down by the watery signs, even in Scorpio. It can operate on high emotion if not contained or channelled properly. Expressed on high-frequency, one that is aware and free of past shackles, Mars, in watery signs, acts from compassion and is informed by inner truth and an overall sensitivity to others.

Mars in Scorpio in the 1st House

Mars in Scorpio likes to work behind the scenes and schemes a strategy before it takes action. It is stealthy but has a regenerative drive that can last a lifetime. Talia Al Ghul, Marion Cottilard’s character in The Batman Rises, is a Mars in Scorpio. In low vibration mode, her plans to take out Batman took patience, waiting in disguise. Her seeds of evil took a lifetime to blossom.

On high vibration, Mars in Scorpio waits until the perfect moment to take action. There are baits along the way to move the plan along. When Mars is in Scorpio in the 1st House, you become stealthy and focus on what you set out to do. This is not usually an impulsive or willy-nilly energy. It watches, it scrutinizes and executes with steely purpose. Having Mars here in Scorpio gives you the stamina, vigour, regenerative power and devotion to self to achieve desires.

With such intensity on the self, you can lose sight of the bigger picture or get too obsessed with one thing, be it a person or a belief—it can turn unhealthy. Scorpio also internalizes and can become vindictive, cynical, suspicious, and judgmental. Awareness is key, as is knowing if your past informs your present actions.

Mars in Cancer in the 1st House

Mars in Cancer is forced to slow down and regulate emotions to balance the energy with the speedy force of action. Trust is earned with this combination. There is an innate need to care for others within their trusted circle. But once Mars in Cancer feels comfortable with someone, they will be fierce protectors and caregivers.

Mars in Cancer in the 1st House seeks comfort in experience. It likes the familiar and is usually held back before it opens up, sussing up a situation. The self provides comfort, and if this is you, you will take action to achieve those means.

This is an excellent position if you want to self-heal and explore your comforts. You will feel motivated to provide for yourself in ways you may never have done before. Mars in 1st House by relocation enables you to self-mother or express your emotions to others more freely and succinctly. It’s a great way to understand your own feelings and desires and what drives them, so you can create boundaries and learn how to nurture others as a result.

Mars in Pisces in the 1st House

Mars in Pisces is the hardest of the three and can make for a difficult way of being. Mars is our energy force; it takes action. Mars in the sign of Pisces is like trying to run in water. The force of action will only be resisted by the strength of liquid. It is a combination that needs to flow with the currents like a Kung Fu master who uses the gravity and flow of water for its benefit.

“Be like water,” and you will learn how to channel the energy through spiritual practices like meditation, art, and music. It is in your very muscles that become your intuition. Mars in Pisces in the 1st house can help you with spiritual practices. To connect directly with your senses through meditation and expressive arts. It’s a great way to balance action and emotional desires. It is also a great way to connect in spiritual and emotional sensitivity with others and your surroundings.

You can become a psychic sponge, however, for better or worse. If there are issues with boundaries, the combination in the 1st House gives you a direct experience, helping you navigate, explore, shape or curate in elevation or in expansive understanding. Mars in Pisces in the 1st House can give you more depth about yourself and your connection to source.
