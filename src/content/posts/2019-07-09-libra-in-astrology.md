## Libra in Astrology sees both sides in equal measure and seeks beauty and balance in all relationships

![Libra in Astrology](/images/uploads/2023/07/SouthNodePost-640x640.png)There is a cool detachment to Libra energy. It’s not the aloofness or the outer realm perception of fellow air sign [Aquarius](https://astrowildchild.com/star-signs/aquarius-the-eccentric-visionary/), but rather an objective observation of its opposites between two polarities.

As a cardinal sign, Libra pushes its agenda onto others. When the goal rides on low vibration, it acts for the sake of winning under the energy of “you” vs. “me.” Coming from the same axis of the independent and aggressive [Aries](https://astrowildchild.com/star-signs/aries-in-astrology/) energy, it can see the other as the rival opposite, naturally reacting in competition, using social charm and grace as a guise to get its way. Once operating on higher currents, Libra, in astrology, sees both sides equally and learns to blend conflicting energies into a steady balance.

Libra’s are energized by social interaction and charged by the currents flowing from others. The neutral Libra air sign is calm and collected, never afraid to express its opinion. Venus-ruled, the energy operates with beauty and grace in mind. It radiates from within and reflects the equality and cooperation expressed in relationships. 

Libra then moves from the “me” to the “we” consciousness. It grows into an awareness of the self and the world by considering others.

Being in cardinal modality and motivated by interaction, Libras pushes for partnership, a concern and mission for the Libra energy throughout its life, all for the sake of harmony.
