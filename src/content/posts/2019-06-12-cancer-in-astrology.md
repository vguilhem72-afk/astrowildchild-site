## Cancer in Astrology is symbolic of the Mother, the home and qualities of emotional intelligence

![Cancer in Astrology Symbol](/images/uploads/2019/06/2.png)Along with the [houses](https://astrowildchild.com/astrology-elements/houses-in-astrology-portals-to-personality-and-reality/), and [planets](https://astrowildchild.com/astrology-elements/planets-the-unique-fabric-of-our-life/), each [sign](https://astrowildchild.com/astrology-elements/we-are-all-the-signs/) vibrates to low and high currents. How we express them depends on where we are in the spectrum. The Cancer energy, at its base, is about nurturing and what comforts. It symbolizes the mother, home life and emotional intelligence.

Cancer energy craves security.  When it feels stable about the details above, the best of the self is radiated. On the flip, feeling threatened forces pincers to be drawn. Neglecting emotional well being can cause them to retreat into their shell, in time, festering in begrudging resentment.

This comes from a sensitive, inherent feeling nature. An energy that feels things out, smells trouble from its heart. Tuning out that whispering pulse can weaken or deactivate its super power of emotional intelligence. Higher vibrating Cancers are the masters of emotions, that is, they are well versed with their own and sensing it in others, as well as the environment.
