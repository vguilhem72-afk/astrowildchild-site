This Taurus Full Moon highlights shocking and surprising news and events. Which can steer us on a a different course than we’ve currently been on. The changes of direction can involve our values, material wealth and the physical pleasures of our five earthly senses.

On the same day, Retrograde Mercury in Libra comes into conflict with Capricorn Saturn, which can place strains on relationships, daily interaction and communication. Limitations may be felt and more responsibilities may weigh down dynamic flow. Looking through an objective lens will be called for. A view which gives us a wider scope of the details involved. A way to see the end goal and the process to get there are keys ways to swim in less turbulent waters.

Overall, the Taurus Blue Moon encourages us to be open to new realities, to consider evolving into new territory, upon a landscape that promises to be charged with new and exciting energy. An experience that may only come around once in a blue moon! Happy Blue Moon!
