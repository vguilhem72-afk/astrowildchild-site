## Full Moon in Aquarius illuminates visionary ideals, setting you out on new trajectories

![Full Moon in Aquarius](/images/uploads/2023/07/forsite-640x640.png) Significant Aspects

-   Full Moon Aquarius °9 — August 1st
-   Square Jupiter °13 inTaurus
-   Mercury opposite Saturn

The Full Moon in [Aquarius](https://astrowildchild.com/aquarius-in-astrology/) helps you break free from stagnation. The difficulty arises when nothing or no one gives way for release, causing a standstill. At the same time, you may have to contend between day-to-day, minute ideas & long-term goals and obligations. Detaching from the situation for a minute can help. But detaching for too long can cause longer than necessary stagnation.

Whether this is happening internally between current feelings and beliefs, or a clash between your view and another’s belief system, it will be about where you can continue to grow while assessing if the attachments are only momentary pleasures with no meaning and value in the long run. You’re being given a wider view with the visionary Full Moon and expanded possibilities in certain areas of life. How do you want to set yourself up? What will your future self thank you for?

The Full Moon By Rising Signs:

Aries: your tribe, hope & dreams

Taurus: career, status, goals

Gemini: higher education, inner and outer travels

Cancer: intimate partnerships, partners resources

Leo: personal & business partnerships

Virgo: daily grind & work, health

Libra: romance, creativity, children, fun & leisure

Scorpio: comforts, roots, homebase

Sagittarius: siblings, immediate environment, social media

Capricorn: physical pleasure, material resources, self-value

Aquarius: self-identity, missions, appearance

Pisces: rest & solitude, meditation, dreamscapes
