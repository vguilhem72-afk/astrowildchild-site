Tonight’s Full Moon is Full of Wisdom, not only because it is in the sage sign of Sagittarius but it conjuncts the Galactic Center at 25 degrees. But before we can fully reap from the knowledge of the Archer, an awakening and release of sort must happen.

Full moons are about culminations and unveiling of truths. In Sagittarius, our aim of vision comes into question and if all our arrows have been pointing to our light of truth. This may have already been simmering to our consciousness when her lunar majesty slipped into the previous sign of Scorpio.

The GC is the milky-looking, pinwheel of energy to which our galaxy spins around—with the centre being a blackhole. The powerful infrared (IFR) vibration is thought to emit evolutionary energy, as it “ provides the release of learned behavior and memories, both negative and positive, all in the interest of moving forward.” (Philip Sedgwick)   

The GC + the Full Moon are working together to bring conscious awareness and growth in a certain area of life.

Additional insight is granted by the square happening between Jupiter and Neptune, as it can show us where we have put much faith into something that may have been veiled in delusion and fantasy.

With much energetic debris stirred, the sextile between Saturn and Neptune could clear the dust  by encouraging us to place faith on realistic and practical solutions.

The opportunity to leap beyond veneers of previous limitations is granted to us with tonight’s lunar activity. As long as we are willing to release, or perhaps redefine what’s now being shown to us under the light of truth, then can we gain the full wisdom of the Sagittarius moon.

Happy Full Moon In Sagittarius!
