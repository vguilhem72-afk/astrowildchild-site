## Mahatma Gandhi Birth Chart Reading. The Peaceful Protestor Who Abstained From The Pleasures of Life. But at What Cost?

A revolutionary force of nature, Mahatma Gandhi protested in non-violent civil disobedience, a stance which advocated against British rule and South Africa and the civil rights of India. By rigorous forms of fasting and taking a 40 year long vow of celibacy, he sacrificed his own health and body for political and spiritual change. Recently, a new biography called “India, After Gandhi”, written by Ramachandra Guha, has been added to the compendium of books about Gandhi. However, unlike what has been previously written about him, the information is laced with much controversy, which chronicles events about his private life and the intimate relationships he maintained with other women, including his teenage grand-niece. A question which urged me to look into his natal chart to see how the revered spiritual leader’s sacrifice, self-control consequently impacted his own carnal desires.

Similar to the transcended Gautama Buddha, Gandhi was born in wealth. With the urgings of his Chief of Minister father, he studied law in London, England and later practiced law in South Africa. A road paved with much difficulties, it was the treatment he received and the inequalities he witnessed abroad that set him towards his journey of compassionate, steady and peaceful resistance.

**Libra Ascendent and Sun**

The words we associate with the man named Gandhi are equality, justice, harmony, resilience, attributes which can also describe the [Libra](https://astrowildchild.com/astrology-elements/libra-in-astrology/) energy, the sign that falls on his [Ascendent](https://astrowildchild.com/astrology-elements/1st-house-in-astrology/) and where the [Sun](https://astrowildchild.com/astrology-elements/the-sun-the-light-of-our-life/) had been when he was born. With his Libra Sun in the spiritual 12th house, and his Moon in [Leo](https://astrowildchild.com/star-signs/leo-in-astrology/) in the [10th](https://astrowildchild.com/astrology-elements/10th-house-our-climb-to-the-top-and-what-we-leave-behind/) house the combination outlines both a political and spiritual leader.

The rising sign shows a persons approach, the mouthpiece or lens of the personal chart. This is how we see the world and the impressions we get are through the qualities of the sign. Having a Libra Ascendent the view is above, objective, seeing both sides. Naturally possessed with initiative force and an advocate of justice and equality, the Libra rising will fight for just causes in graceful, non-invasive ways. Gandhi showed his own persuasive powers through peaceful, cooperative methods.

[He formed the Natal Indian Congress and drew international attention to the plight of Indians in South Africa. In 1906, the Transvaal government sought to further restrict the rights of Indians, and Gandhi organized his first campaign of satyagraha, or mass civil disobedience. After seven years of protest, he negotiated a compromise agreement with the South African government.](http://history.com) 

### _Spiritual leader_

What he paved from his outlook, from a negotiable Libra approach, was nourished from his spiritual purpose, the outer desires coming from his Sun in Libra. Sitting in the higher realm of the [12th house](https://astrowildchild.com/12th-house/12th-house-swimming-in-mysticism-dancing-in-radiance/), opposing [Neptune](https://astrowildchild.com/neptune/neptune-in-astrology-the-divine-within-you/) in the [6th](https://astrowildchild.com/astrology-elements/6th-house/), indicates his soul mission to serve others, develop compassion through everyday rituals (6th house routines) and in meditation and prayer. Anywhere Neptune sits tends to dissolve and inspire. As the house of nutritional health, this area of life became the sacrifice for Gandhi when he went on a hunger strike. With a 12th house Sun, he was in touch with something higher. Wherever our Sun sits is where we thrive.

**Moon in Leo in the 10th house**

Ego, the outer world syncs with his internal needs and expression. His moon in Leo expresses his Sun’s spiritual purpose, his mission in life. In the 10th house it is an expression for the world to hear and thus be inspired by. His inner being, what he is nourished by, is witnessed at large.

[Gandhi drew no distinction between his private and public life. He made his own body a symbol, mortifying it through fasting or marching for political and spiritual change. He even went public with his sexual life — and the negation of it through _brahmacharya_, or chastity.](https://www.nytimes.com/2018/10/10/books/review/ramachandra-guha-gandhi.html)

**Mars, Aries, Lilith and Chiron**

What can cause awry is the inconjunction from the planets in his 1st house of self to Neptune, Lilith and Chiron in Aries in the 6th house.

[In “India After Gandhi”, Guha chronicles the years of Gandhi’s apparent celibacy, self-control and the control he had on others. Some of the most interesting parts of this book concern another group Gandhi sought to instruct: women.](https://www.nytimes.com/2018/10/10/books/review/ramachandra-guha-gandhi.html) 

Which mainly include his wife, and a singer, writer [Saraladevi Chaudhurani](https://feminisminindia.com/2017/11/07/sarala-devi-chaudhurani/) for which he had intimate yearnings, according to letters found. He called her his spiritual wife.

[Yet their letters speak openly of desire — “You still continue to haunt me even in my sleep,” he wrote to her — and he told friends, “I call her my spiritual wife.” He signed his letters to her Law Giver, which, as Guha observes, was “a self-regarding appellation that reveals his desire to have Sarala conform to his ways.”](https://www.nytimes.com/2018/10/10/books/review/ramachandra-guha-gandhi.html)

With the advise of political allies, he ended the relationship, as it would create adverse reactions.

After the death of his wife, he maintained intimate relationships with two of his teenage grandnieces. It was no secret he kept them as companions, walking with him during his marches.

However, according the New York Times, Guha reveals Muha Gandhi, then a teenager, shared a bed with a 77 year old Gandhi, sleeping naked together in the name of his _brahmachyara_ experiments(testing his vows of celibacy). [“Muha was a vulnerable girl who grew up in an ashram where the followers were devotees of the spiritual leader, was adopted by Gandhi and his wife.”](https://www.nytimes.com/2018/10/10/books/review/ramachandra-guha-gandhi.html)

When we ignore the raw nature of our Lilith, it can fester in the shadows, expressing itself in displaced ways. In Aries, it is about the raw individuated self and the need to release such raw force.

Chiron shows our perpetual wounding. One that we must transform by healing others in order to heal ourselves. In Aries, it has to do with self-identity, raw desires, individual action. When he was making love to his wife, shortly after his father had died. The overwhelming guilt he felt, lead him to connect his innate desires and actions to his fathers passing.

His raw nature had turned into his wound.

His _Mercury in Scorpio inconjunct Lilith and Chiron_ can divert his mind into thinking his action appropriate, or to become oblivious about the consequences of his actions at all.

When Lilith is hiding in the shadows, she comes out in other ways. Disrespecting what she truly represents, which is the equality of women and men and the god-given right of expressing a women’s wild nature.
