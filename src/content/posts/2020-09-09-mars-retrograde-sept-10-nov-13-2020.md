The action-oriented planet slows down as the energy stations retrograde today, an inward and reflective journey for each of us in a certain area of life. A period where we’re encouraged to step back, refrain from aggressive force and plan our moves. It can be about reworking current or old projects, or revitalizing relationships for some, while for others it may be about recalibrating the self, by placing efforts, desires and passion into what truly matters.

Things can start to feel stalled and extra challenging, especially with the tension stretched through the retrograde period between Mars in Aries and Saturn, Jupiter, then Pluto in Capricorn, affecting different part of ourselves and areas of life.

Themes to look out for involve conflict between individual desires vs. exterior obligation, duties, & beliefs.  Impulsive action vs. subconscious fears & yearnings, the self vs. authority. The best course of action is to see individual wishes in the bigger picture, making efforts to avoid instant gratification in favour of lasting satisfaction, or transform deep, hidden desires into the catalyst of energy that creates a more solid self. By considering the needs of all sides involved, the challenges become a source of aid to the internal journey with Mars, one that can add a layer of confidence, truth and wisdom.
