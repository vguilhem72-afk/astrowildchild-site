Gemini energy points the way.

With Uranus in one corner and Saturn in another, it’s the second round of tension some of us have already felt in different areas of life. The two planets went at it the first time in mid February, where we saw major stock market  dips and more control via lockdowns, border closure for majority of the world. On a personal level, this would have rocked our comforts, material resources, relationships, as it came into tension with the past, traditions, authority, the status quo.

A square is a catalyst for growth. With Uranus in Taurus involved, it’s growth spurt into the unknown, exciting and authentic on a tangible level. Saturn in Aquarius is growth that builds on the tested and true. We stretch beyond what is known and make it better.  This time around Saturn is retrograde, decreasing tension on some level and giving us a chance to inspect limitations, clear blockages stopping us from moving forward, take authority and rebuild.

It’s about taking the best of the energies have to offer by evolving in a way that is sustained for long-term growth.

We also a bit of love in the sky. The tension further softened by Venus in Cancer. We can tap into its objective and receptive energy, nature that can help make way for diplomacy and cooperation. Mercury also helps tone down conflict by keeping us focus on the moment, our everyday interaction and level of mind and connection to others.

But the real cosmic trident is the Gemini North Node. It’s the bridge that connects the clashing energies. Like Mercury, the energy reminds of our operation on a day-to-day, interacting with a network of people. Being in the NN, it is our collective mission to uphold that. As the sign after Taurus, symbolically, what Taurus plants, what it cultivates on a tangible level is spread to others, perceive through concepts(the mind). Gemini can mobilize the changes currently being set by Uranus in Taurus. The trine it makes to Saturn in Aquarius brings us back to earth, again to our connection, working on a mundane level, funnelling our outer, ingenious vision into our everyday interaction and function.
