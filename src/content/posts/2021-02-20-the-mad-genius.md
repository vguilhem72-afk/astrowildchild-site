## The Mad Genius: How Aquarius, Uranus Dominant, Pluto, Scorpio Strength and Lilith Cardinal Position Indicate Brilliance and The Ways These Rare Gems Shine.

> “I want to put a ding in the universe.” ~ Steve Jobs

What line distinguishes madness from genius?

Obvious one is proving you’re not crazy. Thumbing through history, mania turns into eccentricity when one has something to show for it. But it’s pudding that usually takes a while to whip up and serve. Van Gogh imprinted his madness in art history with his famous mutilated self-portrait. Destitute most of his life, it wasn’t until his death and his stay in a looney bin that his iconic Starry Night, his gifts would find a place down our cultural walk-of-fame.

Persistence then? Dedication and unwavering faith in self and your idea?

Kanye West surely flexes confidence. His work and the associated brilliance continues to grant him street cred and as one of the greatest musician of our time, despite his notorious live antics.

Then there’s timing and luck.

Timing that there is supply and demand, a necessity to make changes in the world. Had Thomas Edison’s mother not home schooled him after having difficulties in a traditional setting—to which he thrived in the independence, the world may not have been illuminated by his genius ideas. Being part of the boom in the industrial revolution, he flourished in an era encouraged for new inventions and innovation. The invention of the light bulb made life more efficient and convenient.

What about divine timing, perhaps? Fate?

Some people were destined to be stars, their bum cushioned with luck, through wealth or strong, rooted upbringing. Making the right friends also adds to ones luck. Kim Kardashian and her sex tape come to mind, along with the entire Kardashian and Jenner Clan fate, their genes, circle of friends and material resources at their disposal helped shift how we consume pop culture.

Without these key solid ingredients ones fate might be bound as an hysteric, blasted nodes of shockwave to complacency, prescribed Zoloft, Xanax, Valium or whatever numbing solution is in vogue. Deviant behaviour forever ostracized, rejected and forgotten. However, baked in self-acceptance from scratch, kneading in your genius self and self-resolved from rejections and unpopularity are likely the staples to reaching brilliance. If you Google the journey that created Amazon and the man behind it, Jeff Bezos, much is said about his difficulties and rejection. It was his unwavering faith in himself ([Jupiter](https://astrowildchild.com/astrology-elements/jupiter-in-astrology/) in Aries, in the 12th) and his product—I’m sure it didn’t hurt to have a team backing him up— that likely pulled him through those rough moments and help the slow success of the now multi-billion dollar Amazon.

The question is what ingenious threads do they hold onto, when life unravels them? What strains keep them on a high? How differently is someone like Steve Jobs cut from the rest of the cultural fabric? What makes the crazy ones, blaze through obstacles, pave roads with their radical ideas without the stigma of insanity? Looking through an astrological lens, down a list of notable figures from past to present, from varying disciplines, woven together by the historical dents made, a pattern was found. The dots connected strong Aquarian and Uranus energy, a chart dominant in cardinal modality, while Pluto and Scorpio being prominent, and, not surprisingly, a pronounced Lilith in a cardinal position.

![The Mad Genius](/images/uploads/2021/03/Einstien-640x638.png)Albert Einstein, an isosceles peg in a square world, failed in grade school. Uranus retrograde in his 3rd house would have made it hard for concentration. His mind a live wire, without the wisdom of age to insulate him, the constant flow of thoughts and ideas had likely distracted and overwhelmed. With Virgo as the expression, he sought to improve navigation down his brilliant and unconventional mind map. The sails of his thoughts, helmed by his natal Jupiter in Aquarius, flew above the mundane and rational and out into the boundless horizons of the 9th house. A life-long mission, envisioned in the most radical sense with a 9th house, Aquarius North Node. That flame burnt bright even under the darkest skies. Lit up from his youth by his Aries stellium (Mercury, Venus, Lilith) and into old age with Saturn keeping the wick alive. Being in the 10th house and with his Part of Fortune involved, reaching his destiny was his luck.

Cardinal Modality Dominant

Deviating from the rest takes a strong understanding of self, or no understanding of how to be anything but. The cardinal houses show what we are being, essentially, just being who we are. It’s part of the 1st quadrant of self, initiation and selfishness. With a strong reliance on self, natives with planets here don’t wait around for anyone to tell them how or when to do it. A strong need to share when 7th is activated, a need to show what you are made of, your accomplishments with 10th house tenants.

![The Mad Genius: Marie Curie](/images/uploads/2021/03/MarieCurieBC-640x635.png)Like Einstein, physicist Marie Curie believed in something different, sharing a natal Aquarius Jupiter, beyond the scope of the norm. Jupiter being in her 1st house equates to a strong sense of self and self-belief, powering her through the opposition she faced from a then male dominant field. She defied the role of a nurturing, domesticated woman, with the help of others, instead nurtured her genius side with Uranus in Cancer in the 7th. Having Uranus charged up in her chart, aspecting her major three and Midheaven, in Scorpionic passion, she was determined to show the world her true nature, right to the core, her identity, her actions, an aggression that helped her acquire fame as a Nobel prize recipient twice.

![](/images/uploads/2021/03/Earhart-640x634.png)American aviator Amelia Earhart shone deviantly bright as well with her North node in Aquarius in the 10th house.
