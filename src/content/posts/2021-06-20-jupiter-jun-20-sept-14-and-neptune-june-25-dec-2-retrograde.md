When we have blockages from the past. When we are not in the moment of flow, not living our truth it’s easy to miss opportunities, distracted, we don’t see the magic that is always around us.

Jupiter goes retrograde in Pisces and Aquarius, shortly after Neptune in Pisces follows suit. Though both planets share a level of beneficial energy, involving acceptance, trust and expansion, the energy can also have us overextend ourselves, create illusions, deceive ourselves and others. With the retrograde period we have the opportunity to look in and find out where that might be happening in our lives. For those in the clear, it’s a perfect time to rediscover our creative nature, hone our intuition, and re-connect to our inner truth, wisdom, wealth. Once the two planets turn in direct motion again, some of us may just witness and seize the synchronistic opportunities, divine love and pleasures abound.
