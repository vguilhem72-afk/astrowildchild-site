The next two days is considered to be one of the best aspects of the year with two of the most beneficial cosmic bodies working in harmony. With Venus in Aries trining retrograde Jupiter in fellow fire sign Sagittarius, we will be gifted in the areas Venus represents: love, relationships, beauty and material objects.

But before we blaze through Aries fervour, the challenging square to Pluto in Capricorn may dull the savoury benefits.  In a short breath of an explanation, let go of old baggage, cleanse that palate clean. To add, the second square to the North Node in Cancer, may cause delay in gratification from another area of life, causing tension between what comforts and nourishes to what is wanted at the moment. The idea with such squares, and with the energies at play, is to reconcile the tension with awareness, giving equal consideration to the both sides. Once dealt with, we’ll find in a certain area of life, the harmonious trine intended to drop us luck and gift us with our hearts desires will taste that much sweeter.
