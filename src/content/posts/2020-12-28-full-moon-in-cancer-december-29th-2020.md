The end of the year wraps with a full Moon in the sensitive sign of Cancer. Full Moons illuminate what was once hidden. They tend to also usher out the old and roll out a rug for the new. While it can also be a time of completion from what was started with the last New Moon, the emotional energy can peak and dip in intensity, especially with old wounds flaring up, an unsavoury past resurfacing. With the air charged with intuitive and initiative power, we’re invited to move into the intensity, embrace it and with Uranus in Taurus creating a harmonious connection, it’s a matter of seeing the light within the wound, to push our way through to the other side, where liberation and truth can be found.

Happy Full Moon!
