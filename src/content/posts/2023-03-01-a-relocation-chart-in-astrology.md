## A Relocation Chart In Astrology and the Difference Between an AstroCartography Chart

![What is a Relocation Chart in Astrology?](/images/uploads/2023/04/2-1-640x640.png)A Relocation Chart is a recalculated natal chart set in a different locale in the world, away from where one was born but in the same moment in time with the same snapshot of the sky.  What changes is your Rising Sign,  the sign at each house cusps and planet positions. The planet sign and degree stay the same, as found in your natal chart.

For example,  if you were born in the US and have your [Rising sign](https://astrowildchild.com/astrology-elements/the-1st-house-in-astrology/) in [Aquarius](https://astrowildchild.com/star-signs/aquarius-in-astrology/), that would change if you relocated somewhere in Europe, say Germany. It would have to be a major change in location to see the difference. If you were born in New York and relocated to Toronto, there would be no significant change since the shift is slight in longitude. The slight changes you might see are the degrees of your Rising sign. However, if you relocated to California, your rising and house angles would change signs, degrees or both. You would also see your natal planets move in a different House.

\*\*\*Ascendent degrees changes depending on latitude and longitude. The MC degree increases about 1 degree of longitude move, decreasing westward.

You can look at the relocation chart as complementary to your natal chart. Your natal chart is an imprint of your natural disposition on the exterior and interior world reflected by occurring transits in the sky. It can supplement your natal chart to give you further support by emphasizing certain planets’ potency in a certain location.

### Significance and Purpose

A Relocation Chart in Astrology can change your energy, experiences and luck in life. It can accentuate and weaken planets. This is determined by the house or angles it sits in your relocated chart. Overall, relocating to a different part of the world can improve and enhance health, romance, and success in career, create happiness and contentment, and give you more focus or a sense of belonging. However, keep in mind that when one or more planets are improved or strengthened, other planets or functions of life are weakened or become less favourable. In other words, we can’t have it all, but getting a proper reading and having a focus can help you find an ideal balance of energy.

#### ![A Relocation Chart vs. an Astrocartography Chart?](/images/uploads/2023/04/4-1-640x640.png)The Difference Between a Relocation Chart and an AstroCartography Chart

A Relocation Chart in Astrology deals with the energy that changes within your birth chart and shows you where the changes are through the modified houses, angles and rising signs. It is a ‘local’ change determined by city.

An AstroCartography Chart is a global map that shows you the concentrated energy within a certain area of the world and will be expressed as the lines of energy (by a planet) on your four angles—Ascendent, Descendent, Mid-Heaven and Imueli Coeli.

For example, Venus’ influence on your Ascendent line will cover more than one country as it influences a whole longitude degree. This will be your dominant or concentrated energy in certain parts of the world. The planet will be of great influence and importance, and the themes the planet represents. According to Erin Sullivan of _Where in the World: Astro\*Carto\*Graphy and Relocation_, “When you deign to live on a specific planetary line, then that unique part of you rises more fully to consciousness.”  It is very significant to work with the agency of the planetary line you are living on or have travelled to.
