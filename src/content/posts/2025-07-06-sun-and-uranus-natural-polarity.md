## **Sun & Uranus Natural Polarity: Uranus in Gemini and Exploring How Your Ego Might Be Blocking Your Evolution**

**Key Elements:**

-   Sun and Uranus Relationship
-   Pluto Aquarius Trine Uranus in Gemini
-   Pluto Leo Generation Transformations
-   Neptune in Aries Enhanced Individuality

**Why is it important to evolve based on Uranus transits?**

-   To move out of a rut 
-   A good ego check-in
-   To contribute authentically in the Gemini Uranus changes 
-   It is the Uranian/Aquarius Age!

No matter where you are in the journey with Uranus changes and growth, it will at some point transit significant personal angles and planets. In a previous post, I covered the ways that [Saturn’s relationship to Uranus](https://astrowildchild.com/uranus-in-astrology/) can challenge you to move into the unknown and the awareness that helps you leap forward. Now, we can look at the Sun, your ego signature, path and purpose to understand blockages to progress and expansion.

What is the Sun in our charts? What does our Sun Sign tell us about our ego identity?

On a physical level, it is what gives us life. Helps all life survive and flourish. Symbolically, our natal Sun is similar in a way that is necessary for our growth and development. It is the essential nutrients that move us forward, keep us motivated, and provide purpose. When we ignore our Sun sign needs, we can lose our way, feel hopeless and lifeless. 

> We carve out our ego identity with our Sun sign expression. 

Uranus is similar in that it represents the part of us that seeks to express individual identity. The house it sits in tells you where and how you see, do or act differently. While Uranus transits reveal which part of your life is ready to evolve with you.

So what is the difference?

The Sun ego identity is grounded in reality. The “I” that separates you from everyone else. Your sign cultivates self-importance, goals, status, career choices within context (house placement) constructed to give you a **sense of belonging.**

Uranus individuality means expressing yourself beyond ego. The energy operates on gut instinct. You act beyond what you’re expected or taught to do. You venture off because you’re called to do so and it feels right. You do it cause it is authentic to you. You can stand your ground **without** **approval** **or people who agree.** 

When you answer a Uranus call, you are moving into the unknown, something that your ego might not like, as it can feel uncomfortable. But you do it anyway and that transcends your ego identity.

It can feel sudden, but that is only because the initial whisper of growth was disregarded. Uranus nudges at first. The sparks of ideas come to light in spurts when Uranus is approaching one of our personal planets or angles. According to Evolutionary Astrologer Jeffrey Wolf Green, there are three Uranian reactions: 1) suppressing it **2)** reckless action **3)** gradual action. The third is ideal. The gradual changes help calculate the risks to move you into the unknown with confidence. 

The aspects indicate the level of ease or challenge. The squares and opposition present themselves as unwelcome changes. The trines and sextile is supportive and the surprise can feel beneficial. Ultimately, it is in your hands whether Uranus pulls the rug out from under you or if you begin to move the furniture around, roll up that rug yourself, sweep up the dust from the layers flying off, and start making changes. 

Similar to the relationship we have with Saturn (societal, cultural, and familial constructs), we tend to fall back on what we know. One of the layers of our Saturn outlines is our ego identity. As I mentioned in a [previous post,](https://astrowildchild.com/uranus-transits-making-the-impossible-possible/) the authoritative planet serves as the gateway between our personal experiences and social interactions. The ringed planet helps us make sense and put order in our lives. How we have built it, what it means and how well it has given us security and a place of belonging. The very reason why people default to suppressing and avoiding Uranus’ calling. 

What you built (Saturn) down your path to give you purpose (Sun) is wrapped up in your ego identity, which can be considered your outer appearance, the path of desires that have given it skin of pride. When we embrace Uranus’ incoming shifts, we break-free from previous ego identity and evolve into a new version that helps serve the collective’s emerging ideals.

### **How it relates to Pluto in Aquarius (Ruled by Uranus)**

Pluto in Aquarius opposes the Leo area of our lives and indirectly our Sun. (more intensely for others if the opposition hits an angle or natal planets). Leo areas will contend with Pluto’s required deep transformations. It will be a clash between self-expression, identity and expressions of community and group connections. Those who resist change and hold on to pride or self-serving intentions will be hit with a dose of Pluto reality, a crisis point.

Pluto is our shared internalized drives. The powerful planet is transforming the collective and how we connect as a community, globally, how we work, and live as humans. It will burn down all that isn’t Pluto vetted to the ground, making things even-steven and reallocating and redefining power dynamics.

#### **Pluto in Leo Generation Transformation**

The largest generation in the last 5O years is the Boomers who belong to the Pluto Leo group (1937-1958). Up until the last few years, we, as a collective, functioned in the ethos of the Pluto Leo Generation.

**Basic shared attitude:** self-importance, hubris, grandeur

**Trends:** materialism, 60’s consumerism, 70’s glamour, 80’s opulence, 90’s celebrity fame.

**The Notions of Status and Power is Shifting: Who Holds it and Who is Losing it. Pluto is helping to Rebuild a New Structure.**

Reflective of Leo characteristics and changes, we see more celebrities outed from their ivory towers. 

Ben Affleck, on the Theo Von show, admitted that it isn’t the same for celebrities as it was when he was coming up in the ranks back in the 90s. Famous people were removed from reality. Their god-like status served their careers well. It kept the illusions of the characters they played. 

Robert Downey Jr. once said, it’s no fun to be famous anymore. There’s no hiding from public scrutiny and criticism. Some of the criticism points to being out-of-touch, power grabs, and even cruelty and evil. Have you followed the Blake Lively controversy? 

We all know that social media and technology have created a platform where everyone can be a celebrity, for better or for worse. Being part of a community, engaging in authentic dialogue and exchange, reflective of podcasts, gives value and traction. People want raw, minute data. Also, anyone noticed how more celebrities are now coming out and sitting in on podcasts?

##### **Neptune in Aries Enhanced Ego and Individuality**

I further question the concept of ego and how it will fit into the new world as we connect in an hyper-augmented reality and ultimately beyond physical reality. Our sense of purpose, our chosen paths, may be less about our ego-driven desires and more about progress toward a connected purpose. 

Neptune in Aries (individuality, ego identity blurred) will redefine our desires, focus and how we connect individually.  The sextile to Aquarius Pluto will stream ideas, changing who we are and what it means. What’s real? What is individual perception? 

Astrologer Jessica Adams believes we will be more community-based, connected by pockets of shared beliefs and linked ideas, rather than a globalized network. Considering how the algorithm works and divides, her points seem valid.

She also says that our faces online (Aries) will be more deceptive. We can be anything we want to be with Neptune’s influence. Again, something we see more and more on our socials and the various avatars we create, as well as how AI personalities appear life-like and perform as humans. 

What about beauty trends? Are we carving out a path that erases or blurs individual appearance? Will it evolve our ego identity?

Beauty practices that were once only affordable for the ultra-rich will become accessible for the masses–the demand is there.

The Uranus in Gemini era is quite important, and understanding how it affects you and your stance on it. How will your ego allow for new modes of operation?
