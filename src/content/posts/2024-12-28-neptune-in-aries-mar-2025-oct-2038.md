### **What is Neptune?**

In Roman mythology, [Neptune](https://astrowildchild.com/neptune-in-astrology/) was the god of the seas. Like its vast waters, Neptune symbolizes our subconscious depth, creative force (water), sublime beauty and the subtle connection of humanity, permeating on a soul level, dissolving and blurring boundaries. Neptune stays in a sign for long periods, influencing a generation born under the sign that the nebulous planet was transiting. Through that sign, they express a collective idealism and a spiritual awakening, often unified by hope, a cause, and even fantasy.

The Cold War was a period of geopolitical tension between the United States and the Soviet Union, and their respective allies, that lasted for decades after World War II: 

-   **Ideological rivalry**: The United States and its allies viewed communism as a threat to individual freedoms, free elections, and free trade. The Soviet Union, on the other hand, sought to spread communism around the world. 
-   **Arms buildup**: Both sides engaged in a massive arms buildup, including the development of nuclear weapons. 
-   **Espionage**: The two sides engaged in espionage against each other. 
-   **Proxy wars**: The two sides supported opposing sides in wars fought by other nations. 
-   **Political maneuvering**: The two sides engaged in political maneuvering against each other. 
-   **Economic aid**: The United States provided economic aid to support its allies.
