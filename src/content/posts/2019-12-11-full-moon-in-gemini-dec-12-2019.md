The Full Moon sheds its light in the curious & expressive sign of Gemini. Full Moon’s are all about clarity & completions.  The Gemini energy is social, logical & analytical.

However, in the next couple weeks, things may not be as clear or logical when it comes to this Full Moon. The illusory & idealistic fog coming from the square to Neptune in Pisces can make things vague, create confusion & sensitivity.

An influence that has the potential to activate the lower energies of the Full Moon in the twin sign, causing irritability, nervous tension or create analysis paralysis. During this period we can become emotionally out of touch & uncertain.

Wherever this is happening in your life best to avoid making any major decisions until the haze breaks.

But if you must navigate the misty path ahead, count on the keen senses of penetrative Mars in Scorpio to cut through the confusion & doubt. Mars in Scorpio is like a well-trained martial artist, unassuming & anticipates every move with a counter move. With the harmonious trine to Neptune in Pisces, it takes on the style called Wing Chun by maintaining flexibility & softness. Under a relaxed vibe, it is a performance of techniques that trains the physical, mental, breath, energy & force to develop Chi.

In other words, finding what keeps you centred, yielding when necessary, & making efforts to understand & work with and NOT against the forces around you, may just be the reactive process needed to clear the air.

Happy Full Moon in Gemini!
