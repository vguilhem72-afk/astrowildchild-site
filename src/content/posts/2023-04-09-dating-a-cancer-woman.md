The world is her womb; she is connected to everything through her incredible instinctual power. The Cancer intuition reminds me of a clip I had seen long ago that went viral for its visceral message of divine love, compassion and our human connection to each other. With her grace, she also had a fearless aura, like a mother protecting her cub. The guy fell into her arms, sobbing while she rubbed his back and hands.
