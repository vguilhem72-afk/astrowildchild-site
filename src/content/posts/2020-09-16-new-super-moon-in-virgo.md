By being present, discerning, focused and attending to the details, this highly manifesting New Super Moon can give fruitful results to new seeds of intentions, ideas and new cycles in at least one area of life.

Pluto, Saturn and Jupiter, all in Capricorn, make a gorgeous trine to this New Moon. To make the most of the his harmonious energy, it’ll be about putting intention to what we truly desire on a deep level, envisioning and strategizing steps for growth and taking authority over the field that can create a bountiful harvest.

 A tension of potent force—likely coming from within, can cause stagnation between different areas of life, as the impulse to make bold moves can feel restrained by outer influences or responsibilities you might have with others. Logic and reason can extinguish the burning will and desire to act. Individuated expression may be thwarted, forced back or silenced by authority or an arching belief.

In order to avoid any unintended mutations, nurture what’s important, what’s worth your time, examine your desires for its longevity. By inspecting, devising and looking at the bigger picture, your seed has a better chance to blossom into perennial growth and satisfactory results.
