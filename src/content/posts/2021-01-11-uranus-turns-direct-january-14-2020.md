## Uranus in Taurus Changes Direction and Energy

Though much of the grand shifts in the last year, on a collective & personal level, can be traced to the energy of the outer planets in Capricorn and Aquarius, it’s been the influence of transiting Uranus in Taurus sparking changes to our ideas of material possession & wealth. This, along with physical comforts & the currency and weight of our values and the impact it has to a certain area of life. Once Uranus went retrograde back in August 2020, it was about re-evaluating, reconsidering & redefining these areas of life.

On a personal level, it was an internal journey about truth, to see where growth is needed, meant to spiral our relationship between our current self & the material world into a more conscious & elevated state.

Now Uranus revs up in direct motion, turning the reflection outward, ideally directing our values, our concept of wealth & physical comforts towards a level of authenticity, a more individuated path, one that points to evolutionary growth.
