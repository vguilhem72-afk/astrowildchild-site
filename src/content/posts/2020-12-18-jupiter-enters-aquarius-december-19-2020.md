Jupiter begins a new journey and starts to work through the sign of the humanitarian, the mind which inclines to innovate. The combination of energy becomes a labyrinth of limitless possibilities, on top of a labyrinth of more possibilities. Jupiter symbolizes culture, faith, beliefs and travel. On the collective front, sweeping changes are abound, new heights will be explored meant to further us into inspired platforms of interaction and connection. But it does start on a personal level. The revolution will happen wherever Jupiter transits our personal charts. Aquarius is a fixed energy, often narrow and obstinate in its focus. Jupiter is about expansion, and sometimes leaving the direction to the winds of intuition and optimism. Balance is always the best option when working with energies stimulated in different direction. It will be about mapping out the voyage into the unknown, holding purpose and logic in our satchels, embracing a compass that always points to our own individuated truths, a trail we mark, to find our way back and share our discoveries, and to implement whatever that purpose may be back onto those we consider our tribe.
