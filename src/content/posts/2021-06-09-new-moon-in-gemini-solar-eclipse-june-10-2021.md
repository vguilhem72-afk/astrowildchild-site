In order to make room for the new, we have to sometimes release the old. During this New Moon Gemini Solar Eclipse, the old will relate to aspects of our ego-identity, fanciful goals and motivations impelled by outside influences. What we are encouraged to release, before we give birth to something new, would have shown itself in the last week, through sudden changes and surprises in a certain area of life. To avoid being overwhelmed, in order to land on our feet, look within to understand the truth, sans the obligation from and expectations of the exterior world.

The wisdom of the Gemini Moon imparts being both receptive and objective.

Eclipses tend to redirect our course and show us where, why or how we have become out of alignment. The current Gemini energy reminds us of what truly excites, what stimulates, what fills our curious tank to get us back on track. Because Mercury is retrograde, it will likely be about re-adjusting ideas, improving perspective, communication and interaction with others to stimulate fresh, new beginnings, then taking action once Mercury turns direct on the 22nd. Look out for Illusions, projections and expectations both coming from self and from others, with the square happening between Neptune and Mercury.

To get the best results, ride the highest vibration of Gemini of flexibility, smart and swift thinking, by taking on light and friendly energy, not holding onto the past, and looking forward in excitement. With an ideal aspect to Saturn in Aquarius and a broader perspective of the period, what you plant now can sprout in thrilling and a growth-oriented future ahead, paved with stability, discipline, mastery and/or long-lasting success. Happy Gemini New Moon Solar Eclipse!
