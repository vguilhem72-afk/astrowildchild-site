## Kardashian-Jenner Klan Birth Charts: A read on the famous family, how they changed pop culture and the connection they all have in their birth chart that have contributed to their fame, fortune and staying power.

> Fan or Foe, the Kardashian—Jenner clan have an undeniably powerful aura surrounding them.

The Kardashian popularity may be do to a carefully curated show and the business sense of momager Kris Jenner, but with that much clout, staying power, beauty and glamour, I started to wonder if the stellar combination had anything to do with luck? I started to wonder if it was all written in the stars? Fascinated by the strong family bond, I was urged to finally look into each birth chart during my own stint with the show. My hunch was correct when I saw the link with their moon, a thread weaving into an intriguing pattern within the family.

The placements of Jupiter for each member of the family showed their wealth and luck, their powerful influence and staying power is noted by their [Scorpio](https://astrowildchild.com/star-signs/scorpio-the-super-healer/), [8th house](https://astrowildchild.com/astrology-elements/the-8th-house-a-well-spring-of-inner-power/) and [Pluto](https://astrowildchild.com/astrology-elements/pluto-in-astrology-owning-our-shadows/) thread. For their beauty and glamour I looked at Venus and [Neptune](https://astrowildchild.com/neptune/neptune-in-astrology-the-divine-within-you/) or [Pisces,](https://astrowildchild.com/pisces/pisces-the-divine-ones/) and of course powerful Kardashian stabilizer was accounted by the strength of their natal moon, the energy of Cancer and the [4th house](https://astrowildchild.com/astrology-elements/the-4th-house-deep-within-us/).

I’d have to start with the woman who started it all, the matriarch [Kris Jenner.](https://www.astrotheme.com/astrology/Kris_Jenner) She has moon in Cancer, very powerful placement as this gives her the raw instinct of a mother, as well as the importance of being one. She definitely knows how to nurture her children, cultivating them to celebrity status and exponential wealth. Now that is what you call rearing at its best—she’s made sure her clutch is well taken care of long after she’s gone. Though the moon in Cancer is considered one of the entrepreneurial signs, we would have to qualify her razor-sharp intellect, her ability to sniff out dollar signs, and aptitude to swim with the sharks in a cut throat business to the energy of Scorpio, its ancient and current rulers.

![](/images/uploads/2019/07/Screenshot-2019-08-22-01.02.55-640x601.png)Kris has a stellium of Scorpio planets and asteroid Lilith in her 2nd and 3rd house. Scorpios know what’s up! She thinks penetratively, she knows how to navigate and even manipulate relationships through communication. Sharp, and doesn’t mess around, she is serious about her third house affairs, which also rules the media. Her values(2nd) are strongly connected to this house. The 2nd house is also where her Sun shines, she identifies with this house, attaching her goals to it.  With Saturn mixed in there, it makes her responsible, hard working, while giving her tenacity and consistency to gain material wealth. The 3rd house is blessed by Venus, giving her luck and charm in communication. She enjoys this part of her life. What we enjoy we work on. With Lilith there, it means it’s where she is rebellious. Not taking any shit! Consequently, this is the house where it all started, with Kim and the infamous sex tape. I’d connect it to Lilith —Kris had no problems exploiting her daughter to the sexual scandal that she knew(Scorponic mom intuition) would start something big. The birth as we now know as the Kardashian kulture.

I think Kim would have made it big no matter what she did. She has lucky Jupiter right on top of her MC at 28° + a second layer of blessings from her natal Venus conjunction. The luckiest planets within close orbs to the MC signifies a continued blessed career. She is immune to any downfall. Of course, things can happen during transits from the malefics, but for the most part, Kim and her celebrity status are pretty safe.

![Kardashian-Jenner Klan Birth Charts : Kim Kardashian](/images/uploads/2019/07/KImKChart-1-640x646.png)

Offset by mostly harmonious aspects, Jupiter is in its downfall of Virgo. It is, however, her self-worth and values that take a hit,  since Venus is also in Virgo, also in its downfall. Kim’s burdens stem from her overwhelming critical nature. But the sign of Virgo tells us that she not only values physical appearance, material things matter as well. Her overbearing self-criticism perhaps drive her to be better. Jupiter expands, and in the sign of Virgo, it can expand self-criticism, never being happy, always wanting to improve, a recipe for good work ethics—according to many interviews, a badge the family wears with pride.

It’s common knowledge that Kim has always loved the limelight ever since she was a child. This can be signified to Neptune in her 1st house, her NN in Leo and most of her planets, aside from her Moon in the 4th, are in the northern “me” hemisphere. Her strong, Virgo work ethic and ample cravings for fame work with a familial Scorpio and Plutonic energy. Kim inherited her moms keen intellect and penetrative communication skills with Mercury in Scorpio, that conjuncts Uranus, giving her rebellious thinking like mommy dearest. (Like mom like daughter—she is certainly the fave—Scorpios tend to not hide favouritism)

Along with the glamour, Neptune also rules illusions and fantasies. The hyper-reality, silicone lifestyle the Kardashian kulture has created is fed to the masses, sprinkles and all.

![Kardashian-Jenner Klan Birth Charts: Khloe Kardashian](/images/uploads/2019/07/KhloeKChart-1-640x696.png)

Khloe’s natal Neptune sits in her 10th house at 29°, telling us that she is celebrity material. She has helped embody the illusory nature of Neptune for the world to see. Pisces rules her second house of values and self-esteem, connecting currency and self-worth to a glamourous, illusionary lifestyle. She knows how to use her Neptune status to her advantage with a Pluto sextile, and is perhaps empowered by it. Jupiter in responsible Capricorn aids this lie or lifestyle with an orb of 6 degree conjunction. This gives her the natural luck and ability to construct a fabulous life style.

Khloe’s Jupiter sits in her 11th house, considered the luckiest house, a fortunate placement she inherited from Kris. But she can hold her own as she too has very powerful Scorpionic and 8th house energy. Mars in Scorpio conjunct Saturn in 8th house+Pluto in Libra also in her 8th house. This indicates a keen aggression and a sense of responsibility in business(8th house).  A very powerful position for Scorpio and Mars, she is juggernaut at creating wealth—a knack she shares with her mom and older sister Kim. The same powerful thread loops into the oldest daughter Kourtney, as well.

![](/images/uploads/2019/07/KourtneyKChart-1-640x658.png)

Kourtney not only shares her sisters outwardly glamour—as Neptune also sits in her 10th house, she has Uranus in Scorpio in the 8th house. Charging her the electrical surging power in business and other people’s money. Pluto neighbours that energy in the 7th house, a powerful place for Pluto as it is in an angled house. She attracts power and wealth in her life and she knows a thing or two about handling business associates, another penetrative power inherited from Kris.

> The Kardashian wealth, luck and glamour crosses over to the Jenner daughters.

Another 10th houser Neptune daughter is Kendall. But she, like her older sisters, isn’t just a veneer of beauty. She has the potent strength in matters of business and accumulating her hearts desires with an interesting stellium in her 8th house tenanted by her Sun in Scorpio and Pluto, as well as her Mars in Sag. Having this energy in the 8th house tells us that it’s not only powerful and she inherited her sisters and mothers keen sense of knowing how to build wealth, but with Sag in there, she has a bit of luck on her side. Not just a slice of luck, Jupiter in Sag conjuncts her Mars in Sag, lending exponential layers of blessings to that house. Jupiter in Sagittarius is strong, showing us that she is fortunate in publishing, writing, communication, that includes all channels of social media and publications. I have a feeling we will always see her face in the media, maybe evolving into words, in her later years.

![](/images/uploads/2019/07/KendallJChart-640x656.png)

The youngest, and according to Forbes, the wealthiest, Kylie is no stranger to the 8th house or Scorpio powers. She has her Moon in Scorpio, her Sun and Venus in the 8th house, again, inheriting the fortunate skills of business and powers of accumulating wealth from her mother. Her NN in the 8th also tells us that she is well supported by the cosmos, as long as she follows her path. Her billionaire status may have something to do with her Jupiter in Aquarius, in her 2nd house of material wealth. Her make-up brand not only tells us about her Neptune sitting in her 1st house—like her older sister Kim, but that she too has the determination and drive to be on top with aggressive Mars(ancient ruler of Scorpio) sitting at her 10th house. Next door, Pluto sits coy in Sagittarius sending powerful luck and optimism from her 11th house.

![](/images/uploads/2019/07/KylieJChart-640x674.png)

Let’s not forget the lone wolf brother Rob Kardashian. He too has Jupiter siting in his second house and in the sign of entrepreneurial sign Aries, reportedly creating wealth through his own sock business. His moon sits in his 8th house— telling us more about his relationship with his mother—he’s instinctive when it comes to accumulating wealth, using his communicative charms with Venus in Aquarius in his first house. Though, his Venus in Aquarius may not have the thirst for or have the desire to follow the celebrity life path like his sisters, no doubt he is influenced by Neptune, having a Pisces Sun and natal Neptune tenanting his 11th house. But perhaps Saturn sitting in his 10th house, limits him to the charming lifestyle, or clears him of the delusions his sister are thickly filmed under. The old dog there, wants to be taken seriously by the world, rather than be seen through illusory fame and glamor.

![](/images/uploads/2019/07/RobKChart-640x679.png)

With the fortune, fame and staying power, there has to be a solid foundation keeping the family unit close. It goes back to home base, to momager Kris, the moon, the 4th house and the nurturing sign of Cancer. A large part of the Kardashian appeal has always been their family values. It seems to be quite legit considering the strength of the moon in each of their chart and the patterns they share.

Rob may not buy into the hyper-reality and drama of the camera, but no doubt part of his luck is in his home base. His Part of Fortune is in the family and homely sign of Cancer, sticking with to this path, creating a life around these energies is where he will always have good fortune.  Mom, as mentioned, earlier, has moon in Cancer, Kim and Khloe have their moon in the 4th house, Kendall has Cancer in her 4th house cusp, Kourtney expands that sign in her life as it is in Jupiter and is exalted. Kylie does not have any of those potent homely energy, but it’s worth nothing that Cancer is at her 7th house cusp and her moon is at her 10th house, placing these energies in an angular house, making them important in her life.

The thread of power, glamour, ingenuity and strong emotional family bonds is obvious within the Kardashian family. It’s the kind of luck that could only be graced by celestial gods, giving them the lure and charm by which the world can’t help but be mesmerized.
