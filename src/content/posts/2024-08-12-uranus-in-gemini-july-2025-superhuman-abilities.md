**“Thinking at a distance”**

A metaphor used in Psychology Today highlights LLMs enabling instant access to vast information and knowledge that push human cognitive bounds.

**What are LLMs?**

_Large Language Models_ are systems currently trained to create AI technological systems. They are “brains” that humans are currently training/feeding historical collective data. Chat GPT, for example, is currently in the 4o model. It collects information from the vast web. The pool of knowledge is made accessible to users by asking specific questions via prompts. The results are instant and can generate things like artwork and literature or assist you in creating an effective schedule or a business plan.

Humans’ current thinking process:

-   linear pathways
-   memory-based
-   logical sense-making
-   hands-on experiences

**Linear vs. Non-linear Thinking**

[**According to a Forbes article,**](https://www.forbes.com/sites/steveandriole/2024/06/19/how-ai-kills-linear-thinking/) “The applications of generative AI are driven by non-linear models.” Real-world data have a complex relationship and are context-dependent, involving images, text, and music and would be difficult to link together via linear thinking. Generative AI relies on what is referred to as _neural networks_ to process information and assimilate patterns to register new streams of data.

Ex. non-linear thinking—consider generating a new song. “A linear model might just string random notes together. A generative AI can learn complex relationships between melody, harmony and rhythm to create something that sounds musical.”

The more LLMs become the norm, integrated into our lives, and the more advanced the tech, the more we see the lines blurring between AI tech and our way of thinking and interacting. We are witnessing its speedy evolution with innovative tech and gadgets being demoed for communication beyond text, with a goal that turns seamless. We will be able to have conversations with LLMs without having our phones on.

Having instant access (thinking at a distance) to something that has happened or is presently occurring elsewhere allows users to be in two places simultaneously. If Chat becomes conversational, if we become integrated with a faster, more equipped system of intelligence, then we can automatically know what is happening on the other side of the world, able to locate a mental connection Marvel’s Professor X style. What occurs is seamless communication and interaction. We are birthing a co-evolution and our mind’s optimization as the lines blur between our own thinking capabilities and our use of  of AI tech.
