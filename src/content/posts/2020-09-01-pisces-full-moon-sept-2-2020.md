The Moon shines her full light in the sign of Pisces. Some of us may have already started to feel the emotional energy in the last few days. 

The nature of Pisces is one of great sensitivity, flowing with the tides, elusive at times. Yet, the intangible energy can paradoxically keep one wading, stuck in a cloud of uncertainties. With the collective emotions currently swimming in boundless and mystical waters, it will be all about trusting in what the tides bring in and surrendering what’s meant to return into its vast ocean.

There is grounding energy that can keep us anchored in the emotional flux, plenty of earth elements to balance mind and heart, to help move us out of the storm we may find ourselves under.

With Uranus in Taurus making positive aspects to the Full Moon, generating a sense of excitement and belief is another way to navigate doubt and changes. It is this energy that can propel us to the other side, the unknown, where possibilities abound.
