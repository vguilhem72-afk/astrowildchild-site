A window of opportunity opens up for each of us to focus on what’s needed to be examined right down to its authenticity. Uranus, currently in Taurus, joins the other power players in retrograde motion, having us reflect on values, the comforts of our material world and change the fate of a certain area of life.

If you’ve already been dropped high voltage shocks, received unwelcoming news, think of it as a wake-up call for long overdo change. When we’ve stalled for too long, ignored the nudges that lead to growth, it is Uranus that zaps us out of our funk, out of stagnation. It is the area of life or a part of us that has likely become inert, complacent, perhaps even indulgent(Taurus energy). And if you pay heed and ride the electric currents of change, you may recognize the twist of fate for its silver lining.

> For the rest of the year, we are encouraged to be bold, innovative, shock ourselves into doing what we would never do and light up the road less travelled.

By the time Uranus turns direct, mid Jan 2021, the truths discovered, the revolutionary changes within should start to spark outward and flow into manifestation.
