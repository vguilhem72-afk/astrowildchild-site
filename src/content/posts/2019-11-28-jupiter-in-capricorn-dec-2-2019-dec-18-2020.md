The energy of luck and opportunity makes a major shift as Jupiter stations direct tomorrow. The king of the gods changes from its home sign of flourishing and optimistic Sagittarius to the realistic & constricting sign of Capricorn. Capricorn being under the reign of disciplinary Saturn is about structures, rules and sticking to them. Jupiter is about reaching beyond your limits, what’s expected and the freedom that comes with it.

Opposite in energy, there’s nothing worse for the Jupiter energy than restricting expansion, limiting abundance and curbing enthusiasm. But like anything in life balance is always good.

Wherever Jupiter transits in our life, we are called to focus, plan and see it through the end in order to get the full benefits and bounty of the sky god. It’s about working with the conservative, goal-oriented Capricorn energy, in Jupiter style with optimism, faith, love and wisdom.

Where will you be feeling the awkward, yet effective Jupiter in Capricorn energy? Based on your Rising, Moon & Sun?

_**Aries**_: your truth & wisdom, long-distance travels, publishing to a wider audience

**_Taurus_**: deep intimacy & pleasures, partners resources, hidden self

**_Gemini_**: business & personal relationships, open-enemies

**_Cancer:_** daily work & grind, health, employees

**_Leo:_** romance, children, creativity, leisure & fun

**_Virgo:_** comforts of home, parents, roots, whatever nourishes

**_Libra:_** communication, siblings, immediate environment

_**Scorpio:**_ material resources, physical pleasures, self-worth

**_Sagittarius:_** self, appearance, lens on life

**_Capricorn:_** subconscious, dreamscape, rest & solitude, meditation

**_Aquarius:_** tribe, hopes and aspirations, benefactors

**_Pisces:_** career, status, ambitions
