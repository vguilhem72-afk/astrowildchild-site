For the next few weeks, well into September, the positive currents of Virgo Venus transit is at a high, as we’re charged with support from cosmic friends.

Right out of the gate, Venus will make sweet conversations with the Moon in Taurus, aligning love, emotions & sensual desires. The two cosmic lovers of the sky, Mars & Venus, sit cozy next to each other for about a week, with an exact conjunction on Aug 24th, making for feel good relationships &  bedroom satisfaction, as it will be about servicing the other, while considering improvements within the bond. It could also mean feminine & masculine energy working together within, putting action into what you want.

The cosmic lovers continues to surge in poetic juice while the duo trines Uranus in Taurus, as it can mean turning the normally reserved energy of Virgo into new and exciting ways of pleasures, or a sudden download of delightful feelings, boosting self-value, motivation & physical drive.  The convo, mid-month with Jupiter in philosophical Sagg creates contrast between our highest ideals & beliefs with current values—ones grounded in practical matters & responsibilities. Count on Saturn Retro in Capricorn to remind us what we’ve rebuild to date and to let the inner authority we’ve cultivated so far  call the shots.

Before Venus moves into Libra mid September, its transit in Virgo will receive some intensity with Pluto in Capricorn retrograde. In particular areas of life, what we we’ve transformed or where we’ve deeply healed can now translate into passionate joy, deep bonds and luscious gratification.
