## **Why You Always Fail at Love: It's Not One Thing. The Trials And Trails**

Failing at love is rarely about bad luck or bad people. It’s usually a pattern. And the pattern shows up in your birth chart.

It’s also rarely one thing. Love problems work like dominoes. It often starts with Venus: not embracing what you truly value and how you want to be loved. Then Mars takes over, chasing desire and instant gratification over what lasts. Then the 7th house shows you the result: the partners you attract and the patterns that repeat.

None of the placements below mean you’re doomed in love. A block is information. It shows you where the work is. Each placement comes with the block, the path through it, and an insider tip on what makes it better or worse.

One note before you start: no placement works alone. A difficult Venus can be balanced by a supportive Moon. A heavy 7th house can be softened by a strong Jupiter. Read your chart as a whole.

### **VENUS — What You Value, How You Love, What You Attract**

**Venus in Virgo**
_The block:_ You’re waiting for the perfect partner, so no one ever measures up. Love gets critiqued before it can grow.
_The path:_ Love through devotion and small daily acts. Choose someone who improves with time, not someone who arrives flawless.
_Insider tip:_ Check Mercury, Virgo’s ruler. A strong Mercury helps you communicate needs instead of criticizing. A Jupiter aspect softens the perfectionism. A Saturn square makes it harsher.

**Venus in Aries**
_The block:_ You love the chase and lose interest once it’s caught.
_The path:_ Keep the adventure alive inside the relationship. Partners who challenge you hold your interest longer.
_Insider tip:_ A Saturn aspect gives this Venus staying power. A Uranus square makes the restlessness stronger.

**Venus in Scorpio**
_The block:_ All or nothing. Jealousy, intensity, testing your partner to see if they’ll stay.
_The path:_ Trust gets built, not tested. Let depth develop gradually instead of demanding it upfront.
_Insider tip:_ A Jupiter trine softens the intensity. Hard aspects from Pluto amplify the control and jealousy.

**Venus Retrograde at Birth**
_The block:_ Repeating old patterns. Going back to exes or dating the same type over and over.
_The path:_ Study the pattern before the next relationship. What you return to shows you what still needs healing.
_Insider tip:_ The year your progressed Venus turns direct is often a turning point in how you love and who you attract.

**Venus Combust (close to the Sun)**
_The block:_ You lose yourself in the partner. Your needs get overshadowed.
_The path:_ Keep your own identity inside the relationship. Your interests, friends and goals stay yours.
_Insider tip:_ If Venus is cazimi, within about 17 minutes of the Sun, the opposite is true. Your love nature is purified and highlighted.

**Venus in the 12th House**
_The block:_ Secret, unavailable or sacrificial love. Falling for people you can’t fully have.
_The path:_ Bring love into the open. Choose partners who can show up publicly and consistently.
_Insider tip:_ If Venus is within about 5° of your Ascendant, it acts like a 1st house Venus. Love becomes visible and easier.

**Venus Conjunct Chiron**
_The block:_ The “I’m unlovable” wound. Settling, or sabotaging love before it can hurt you.
_The path:_ Heal the worth wound first. Once you believe you deserve love, you stop accepting less.
_Insider tip:_ A Jupiter trine speeds the healing. A Saturn square deepens the wound but builds lasting strength once worked through.

**Venus Square or Opposite Saturn**
_The block:_ Fear of rejection. Guarded, emotionally careful. Love tends to arrive late.
_The path:_ Let love build slowly. Small consistent steps feel safer than grand gestures.
_Insider tip:_ This placement improves with age, especially after your first Saturn return around 29. Late love with this aspect is often lasting love.

**Venus Square or Opposite Neptune**
_The block:_ Idealizing partners. Rose-colored glasses, falling for potential, rescuing.
_The path:_ Date the person in front of you, not the version you imagine. Watch actions over time.
_Insider tip:_ A Saturn aspect to Venus adds the reality check this placement needs.

**Venus Square or Opposite Pluto**
_The block:_ Obsessive love, control, power struggles.
_The path:_ Balance power in the relationship. Intensity is fine. Control isn’t.
_Insider tip:_ A trine from Jupiter or Saturn helps you channel the intensity into deep, transformative commitment. Mars involvement makes the power struggles sharper.

**Venus Square or Opposite Uranus**
_The block:_ On-again off-again love. Freedom versus closeness. Drawn to the unavailable.
_The path:_ Build a relationship with space in it. You need a partner who values independence as much as you do.
_Insider tip:_ A Saturn aspect stabilizes this placement. Trines from Uranus suggest you thrive in unconventional partnerships rather than struggle with them.

**Venus Unaspected**
_The block:_ Disconnected from what you actually want in love.
_The path:_ Get clear on your values before you date. Write them down.
_Insider tip:_ Look at the planet ruling Venus’s sign. That planet tells you how your Venus actually operates. Transits to an unaspected Venus tend to hit hard and fast, so watch them.

#### **MARS — Desire, Attraction and Instant Gratification**

**Mars Stronger Than Venus**
_The block:_ You chase desire over values. Instant gratification wins over lasting love.
_The path:_ Let Venus choose and Mars pursue. Decide what you value first, then go after people who match it.
_Insider tip:_ A harmonious aspect between Venus and Mars helps desire and values work together.

**Venus Square Mars**
_The block:_ What you want and what you value pull in opposite directions.
_The path:_ Name your values before attraction takes over. If someone excites you but doesn’t fit your values, notice it early.
_Insider tip:_ A Jupiter aspect to either planet eases the tension.

**Mars in Libra**
_The block:_ People-pleasing. Not asking for what you want.
_The path:_ Practice direct requests in small moments. Your needs are allowed in the relationship.
_Insider tip:_ Check Venus, Libra’s ruler. A strong Venus helps this Mars assert itself gracefully.

**Mars in Cancer**
_The block:_ Passive-aggressive. Emotions come out sideways instead of directly.
_The path:_ Name feelings early, before they build up.
_Insider tip:_ Check your Moon, Cancer’s ruler. A well-supported Moon makes this Mars protective rather than defensive.

**Mars Conjunct or Square Pluto**
_The block:_ Intensity that turns into power struggles.
_The path:_ Channel the intensity into shared goals instead of each other.
_Insider tip:_ A Saturn aspect gives this placement self-control.

**Mars Retrograde at Birth**
_The block:_ Suppressed desire. Hesitating to go after what you want.
_The path:_ Get clear on what you want privately first. Then act on it.
_Insider tip:_ The year your progressed Mars turns direct often marks a shift in how boldly you pursue love.

##### **THE 7TH HOUSE — Partnership and Commitment**

**Saturn in the 7th**
_The block:_ Delays, fear of commitment, heavy partnerships.
_The path:_ Commit to building something real over time. This placement often brings older partners or later marriage that lasts.
_Insider tip:_ Your Saturn return around 29 is often a major relationship turning point. A Venus trine softens the heaviness.

**Neptune in the 7th**
_The block:_ Illusion or deception. Seeing who you want instead of who’s actually there.
_The path:_ Date slowly. Verify. Let time reveal who someone is.
_Insider tip:_ A Saturn aspect to Neptune grounds this placement.

**Uranus in the 7th**
_The block:_ Unstable partnerships. Sudden starts and sudden endings.
_The path:_ Choose a relationship structure that allows independence.
_Insider tip:_ A Saturn aspect brings stability without killing the freedom.

**Pluto in the 7th**
_The block:_ Power struggles and transformative, sometimes destructive, relationships.
_The path:_ Keep power balanced. Therapy and honest conversations help.
_Insider tip:_ A Jupiter trine turns intense partnerships into deeply transformative ones.

**Chiron in the 7th**
_The block:_ The partnership wound. Old hurts replay in each relationship.
_The path:_ Heal the original wound, often from early family dynamics.
_Insider tip:_ Aspects from Venus or Jupiter speed the healing.

**Mars in the 7th**
_The block:_ Conflict-heavy relationships, or attracting combative partners.
_The path:_ Learn healthy conflict. Active, driven partners suit you when the fighting is fair.
_Insider tip:_ A Venus aspect softens the conflict. Saturn in supportive aspect gives consciousness, deliberation and restrain to action.

**South Node in the 7th**
_The block:_ Losing yourself in the partner. Codependency.
_The path:_ Your North Node in the 1st asks you to build your own identity first. Be whole on your own, then partner.

**7th House Ruler in the 12th**
_The block:_ Hidden, secret or self-undoing relationships.
_The path:_ Bring relationships into the open. Watch for self-sabotage.
_Insider tip:_ If the ruler is Venus or Jupiter, this placement is far less damaging.

**7th House Ruler in the 8th**
_The block:_ Intense, crisis-driven partnerships.
_The path:_ Pair depth with stability. Choose intensity that builds rather than destroys.
_Insider tip:_ A Saturn aspect adds stability. 

**7th House Ruler Afflicted by Saturn, Neptune or Pluto**
_The block:_ That planet’s lesson aimed directly at your partnerships.
_The path:_ Work the lesson. Saturn asks for commitment, Neptune for clarity, Pluto for balanced power.

###### **THE 5TH HOUSE — Dating and Romance**

**Saturn in the 5th**
_The block:_ Romance feels like work. Fun gets blocked.
_The path:_ Schedule fun. Let romance mature over time.
_Insider tip:_ A Jupiter aspect lightens this placement.

**Neptune in the 5th**
_The block:_ Fantasy romance. Falling for potential.
_The path:_ Date reality. Put the fantasy into creative work instead.
_Insider tip:_ A Saturn aspect grounds the fantasy.

**5th House Ruler in the 12th**
_The block:_ A hidden or confusing dating life.
_The path:_ Get clear on what you want before you date.
_Insider tip:_ If the ruler is Venus or Jupiter, private romance can still be happy romance.

**THE MOON — Emotional Needs**

**Moon in Capricorn or Scorpio**
_The block:_ Emotionally guarded. Hard to let anyone in.
_The path:_ Let people in gradually. Trust is earned in layers.
_Insider tip:_ A Moon trine to Venus or Jupiter softens the guard.

**Moon Square Venus**
_The block:_ Your emotional needs and what you’re attracted to don’t match.
_The path:_ Date for your needs, not just your attraction.
_Insider tip:_ A Jupiter aspect to either eases the conflict.

**Moon Conjunct, Square or Opposite Saturn**
_The block:_ Fear of vulnerability. Emotional coldness.
_The path:_ Practice vulnerability in safe relationships first.
_Insider tip:_ This placement often softens with age and experience.

**Moon in the 12th**
_The block:_ Hiding your feelings, even from yourself.
_The path:_ Journaling or therapy helps you find what you actually feel.
_Insider tip:_ If the Moon is close to your Ascendant, your feelings become easier to express.

**LILITH**

**Lilith Conjunct Venus or in the 7th**
_The block:_ Rejection patterns, or attraction to taboo or unavailable partners.
_The path:_ Own your desires without shame. The right partner accepts all of you.
_Insider tip:_ A Jupiter aspect helps you embrace this placement instead of fighting it.

**The Bottom Line**

Love doesn’t fail randomly. It follows the pattern in your chart. Find the first domino: usually Venus, sometimes Mars, the Moon or the 7th house. Fix it, and the rest start falling the right way.
