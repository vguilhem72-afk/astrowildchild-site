Mars in Aries is currently in reflective, retrograde mode, making sweet exchanges with Venus in Leo. While the Sun in Libra creates mutual reception to Venus, a harmonious connection helping to quell the intensity and aggression that may be felt during the Aries Full Moon. Wherever this is happening for you, there is a balance of yin-yang energy in the air, giving us good judgment on when to use force and when to step back. We can express emotional needs with ego desires, ease conflicts between our internal and exterior world in courageous, calm and creative ways.

The wounded healer, Chiron, currently in Aries, is also highlighted, which can trigger an unhealed past, associated with self-identity, individuality, previous ill-actions, or unmet desires. With symbiotic links above influencing us below, it is the salve of self-love and graceful action that can help soothe our open and inflamed wounds. Happy Aries Full Moon!
