An energetic and fierce Full Moon in Aries, our actions and will come to light, how we react and how well we use force. The sign of Aries is known for its individualistic nature, bravery, while being frenetic and selfish at times.

With a challenging sky in the last few days, acting on the lower vibes of the sign may have felt more convenient. It is an energy shaken up by the opposition to the Sun in Libra, creating tension to our outer world and inner reality. Themes that can involve home versus work, emotions versus logic. Which can be aggravated by Pluto in Capricorn, causing control issues, power struggles—triggering hidden fears and traumas. Mars is currently in Libra, a sign that can hold back the natural aggression and force of the warrior planet. In this case, can be beneficial to think things through in objectivity, while considering the other side. Depending on our own level of fiery intensity, this can stoke passive-aggressive tactics and manipulation to push selfish agendas.

To offset conflict, tune into expanded tolerance and optimism vibrating from the Neptune in Pisces and Jupiter in Aquarius. The harmonious sextile from Neptune in Pisces can help buffer impact between Pluto and Mars’ forceful dominating nature. It’s about tuning into the energy of compassion, empathy and acting from love.

The positive aspect between Jupiter in Aquarius and the Full Moon can help us see beyond our selfish needs, and respect individuality, which can point toward common ground and goals. Transit Venus is in Sagittarius, an energy we can all tune into to help stretch love beyond our own boundaries.

A Full Moon cycle normally ushers in changes and renewal. Let go of what cannot be controlled and understand what is within our own power. Being in the energy of Aries, it points to ourselves, our individual nature and how we act. Self is the only thing we can control. Knowing this, along with the positive vibes available to us, may just make it that much easier to bravely act from love, in spite of fear.
