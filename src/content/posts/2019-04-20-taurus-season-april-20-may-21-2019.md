 A General Overview

The sprite, highway chase of Aries energy turns into cruise control with the Sun now in Taurus.

But the need for speed will still be felt with Venus in Aries, this time influencing the things we want, maybe even turning up the heat between the sheets! Our mind can now pace in lightening bolts, hastiness trumping patience with Mercury also in the fiery sign. Saturn going retrograde before the end of April can offset this crackling energy, as normal pressures of demand and duty will lift in certain areas of our life. The chill vibes will continue with the New Moon entering Taurus on May 4th and with Venus entering Taurus— its ruling planet—mid-May, heightening our sense of pleasure. Just know that Taurus season can make us indulgent by spending more, eating loads, even making us lazy. Channel Taurus’s highest form of beauty, its spiritual nature. It’s the sign that brings the spirit back on earth, seeing it in every budding flower, feeling it from the touch of a lover, smelling it in the spring air. It is an energy present in its five senses, breathing the magic of every moment.
