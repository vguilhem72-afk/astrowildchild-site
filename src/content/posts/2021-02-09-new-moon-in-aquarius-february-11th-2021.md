Sweet notes also permeate the air, with Jupiter and Venus conjunct in the Water Bearer sign. This can bring luck and opportunity to whatever we set our intentions towards. What we plant now can exponentially bloom into a source of joy, pleasure and abundance, especially in relationships, finance and beauty-related goals.

At the same time, the temperature may rise with Aquarius Saturn and Taurus Uranus growing in tense connection. Causing resistance and inflexibility on one side, while experiencing over-reactions, irrational and impulsive moves on the other. To circulate the air, uproot what no longer is in symbiosis, trim what’s lost its benefits, so to give passage for exciting growth and fresh opportunities.
