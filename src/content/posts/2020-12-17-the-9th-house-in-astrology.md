## The 9th House in Astrology stretches our minds, perceptions at different angles to ultimately formulate our own personal truths.

![The 9th House in Astrology](/images/uploads/2020/12/10-640x580.png)

We rise from the raw, cold dungeons of our [8th House](https://astrowildchild.com/2020/11/15/the-8th-house-a-well-spring-of-inner-power/) with deeper knowledge of self to the higher meaning and purpose of it all in the 9th House. It is where we have a broader view and start to form our philosophy, principles and mores through our experiences from the last eight Houses. The 9th House stretches our minds and perceptions at different angles to ultimately formulate our own truths.

Where the opposite [3rd House](https://astrowildchild.com/2020/06/15/3rd-house-strutt-your-stuff/) is the immediate experience, familial, local and our daily interactions, in the 9th, we move away from the mundane and see our life patterns to distinguish our role in the world.

It is the House of higher education and foreign cultures, where we can travel physically and in the mind to learn and experience from the unknown. It is through another’s eyes, a journey of the vast world beyond our own, that we widen our experience. From the 3rd House of logic, from facts to symbols, we transcend into ideas, concepts, an abstract view we assimilate from our journeys and the world around us. Broadening our perspective, we adhere to certain mores that draw hope and inspiration.

Writing and publishing also connects to the 9th House. We share our individual experiences with others through communication and interaction to spread our own inspiration and hope to others.  What we learn in the 9th House becomes the wisdom to live our most ideal life.

The sign at the cusp shows the flavour in how we translate the mundane, our experiences, into its highest meaning. The fire signs will emphasize beliefs in identity. Purpose will lean towards passions and creative will. The airy light of truth will be found in sharing ideas and stretching the mind. Water elements are coloured by emotion, purpose, and truth revealed in the unity to life, the profound and inner workings of the self. The earth signs will rationalize meaning and purpose. To survive, to live an ideal existence is to live on a practical level.

The planets signify how we take action with our grand ideas and can indicate how we interact with those of foreign cultures. The interactive planet [Venus](https://astrowildchild.com/2020/10/07/venus-the-choices-we-make/) here will see things in a pleasurable way. Relations with others are cordial, even lucky. Saturn, on the other hand, views 9th House matters with caution and cynicism. The limitless sky can be considered a burden to Saturn’s goals and plans.

[Jupiter](https://astrowildchild.com/2020/12/06/jupiter-in-astrology-inherent-truths/) sits pretty in its own domain, has a vast mind and is inclined to accept life as is, evolving in and seeking knowledge and truth through encounters. Pluto, in the expansion of the 9th, will have a regenerative element. The native grows in ideology and beliefs in response to changes and transformation. A belief system coloured by Pluto can be passionately attached to a view, which, over time, requires a process of extraction. Similar to Pluto, but on a frequent and abrupt level, overall views can shift with Uranus in the 9th. As an individualistic planet, walking off the beaten path, ideas can be shocking, even unethical to some.

The 9th House sheds light on our experiences, helps us form the framework of our beliefs and principles, and overall path by assimilating beyond logic and the everyday. Preceding the 10th House of career, goals, status and legacy, it is the pillars of our 9th House ideals and mores that help us define what honour means to us. We go back to our 9th House for an overall view of our journey, giving us a wider perspective, a personal map surveying meaning and purpose and charting our individuated truth.
