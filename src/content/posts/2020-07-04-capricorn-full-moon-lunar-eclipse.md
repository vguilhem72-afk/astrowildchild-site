> **A Capricorn Moon likes to get things done, has a blueprint in hand and the emotional drive to succeed.**

As with all Full Moon’s there can be clarity gained, things can come to fruition, while endings make room for new beginnings. Being a Lunar Eclipse there can be major turning points, aligning you to a more authentic path.

Being in detriment, the Moon isn’t the happiest in the sign of Capricorn. An out of place, weakened energy, turn of events, what has been coming to a head will likely cause internal discomfort. There can be emotional flux, & a sense of melancholy can take place. On the flip, it’s a nourishing energy on a practical level and should make sense in the bigger picture.

![](/images/uploads/2020/07/images-1.jpg)

With the Moon and the Sun opposite each other, there is usually a balance needed with self vs. others, emotion vs. reason. In the Capricorn & Cancer energy axis, steadying emotions may require you to step back and assess the situation for its longevity or examine the long-range goal, while measuring the emotional investment involved.

The harmonious trine to Uranus in Taurus encourages surrendering, and inviting the unknown, as it will be to your benefit. The sky indicates positive changes & lucky breaks manifesting on a tangible level.

In order to get the most of the phenomena, ride the rhythmic cosmic tides by keeping your emotional needs framed in reality, by taming instant gratification with what will give lasting satisfaction and by welcoming exciting surprises ahead.
