The Square to Pluto in Capricorn July 23’23 is to have you understand your power, to teach you how to rise even higher. The move into Aries quickly brings challenges when the Nodes square Pluto in Capricorn. Breathe. You may become faint at what you discover. The fulfillment of the North Node in Aries is about individuality, desire and drive–parts of you that you’re encouraged to refine at its highest expression to land on your feet, sustain solid ground as Pluto’s tectonic shifts happen in the next 20 years in Aquarius. The NN cycle in Aries and SN in Libra will make the process easier with all the work done prior, which will be the foundation to build something worthwhile in the areas of your Aries and Libra chart. This includes your relationship houses (1st, 7th, where Venus is and Mars sits).

Both planets are spiritually focused on connecting the collective into cohesion—for better or worse. Saturn in Aries will encourage you to look at the big picture to understand and outline your unique energy and how you can survive and reinforce your place in the major collective shift.
