The Libra Full SuperMoon highlights relationships, ones we have to ourselves, others & the parts of our lives. A nourishing energy of balance & objectivity. A light shining grace & diplomacy between our wants & needs, outer obligations & inner desires.

However, it is the square to Jupiter & Pluto that can tip the golden scales of Libra. A subtle yet potent energy, conflict that arise can trigger deep wounds, buried needs, suppressed inner obligations, ones bound up by the past, old beliefs & low-key conditionings. As a pushy(cardinal) active energy that rides on low vibrations, it can make for passive aggressive actions, bending when we shouldn’t & neglecting our own needs, straining emotions to unhealthy extremes.

Actions can also turn stubborn & impulsive, an influence from the Aquarius Mars & Taurus Uranus square, causing aggression, a spittle of callous & cold words. A view that can turn detached & condescending, & undermine self-currency.

Thankfully, the sky is also lit with positive energy, with Pisces Mercury, Gemini Venus & Aquarius Saturn significantly involved.

The Mercury in Pisces sextile energy can soften through reason & compassion. The lens of love & sympathy can slow the teeter totter effect.

It’s also about taking efforts to look at a situation from an individual standpoint, yet, seeing our own hand in the mess, & equating it to the entire picture. Aquarius Saturn means taking responsibility & being accountable for our own actions. Which, in turn, can not only lead to self-authority, it also contributes to our own evolution, helping to understand ourselves in a new context.

Venus trine Mars energy can balance yin & yang energy, when to be sensitive, receptive & when to take action & be willful. The harmonious reception currently happening with Venus & Saturn helps us focus on what is important & realistic. A stance that takes consideration of all those involved, ultimately helping to restore equilibrium once again.
