Neptune joins the other cosmic power players in a retrograde dance.  Being in its home sign of Pisces, the qualities symbolized by Neptune are heightened and in a personal chart, this is where we feel a stronger than usual dream like quality to the nature of that house, where we can tend to go with the flow of things or where we can source a well of inspiration.

On the flip, it can be where we lack discipline, where we can fall into escapist tendencies or where we can be cloaked in delusions and idealism.

How well you’ve expressed the sea God’s energies will reflect the ease and quality of the retrograde melody—a discordant reality check, a retraining and reworking in a certain area of life or a harmonious beat you naturally ride on, where creative play is at your download, an opportune time to channel into the imaginative resources Neptune offers.

Though the movements may be awkward, accept the hand from Jupiter as he makes the square to Neptune twice during this period. Each step will highlight where you’ve misplaced faith & swelled optimism. Your motions will be much smoother with Saturn & the sextile to Neptune. The duet gives faith to each thorough steps taken and the time and effort put into them.

By the time Neptune turns direct—wherever that maybe in our own life—our feet should start dancing to the right notes, inspired into a new vibration, where our entire being flows into a more natural rhythm.
