This will be an energetically charged Full Moon, laced with a bit of unsavoury moments but topped with lucky ones as well.

A Full Moon is about endings, seeing things clearly, while we’re stretched between our personal & outside world or feminine & masculine aspects. In Aries, it can become intense, self-serving, driven to compulsion & full of will & determination.

The Full light or a completion of sort will shed a showdown between the Full Moon & powerhouse Pluto in Capricorn. Pluto is about the low key, secrets, it works on a subconscious level, forcing change & rebirth. A square is when two foreign energies meet each other in tension, yet can be wrapped in a silver lining of growth.

The two pushy & clashing energy can emotionally test our will, our determination, our individual needs against authority, ambition or the work we do. The potential to react, become irrational or selfish is likely.

The emotive Aries against the power of Pluto reminds me of an unstoppable force colliding with an immovable energy. If there’s anything you need to know about Pluto, it is an energy to be reckoned with. Wherever it is happening in your life, best to step back—that includes our ego. A resistant to pull the trigger may be difficult, but surrendering is key. The opposing Sun in Libra to the Full Moon blazes its own drama into the battlefield. Though, it means a testing of our core, our ego, our goals as extremely as our emotions, & can tempt us to steam in passive aggressive words or underhandedly push our charm, this is also the part of us that has the potential to become detached, to get an overview of the situation to act in grace & diplomacy.

Fortunately, before there are any casualties involved, the Lunar trine to Jupiter in Sagittarius rides in to enforce a sense of internal law, as we see the positive side to the situation or a necessary truth. It can also mean luck, opportunity or abundance radiating from another area of life, compelling us to let go, to retreat intact, perhaps with new skin, towards a sunnier horizon of progress and joy.
