## New Moon In Gemini June 2023

![New Moon in Gemini June 2023](/images/uploads/2023/06/1-640x640.png)The New [Moon](https://astrowildchild.com/moon-in-astrology/) in [Gemini](https://astrowildchild.com/gemini-in-astrology/) will urge movement from ideas, thoughts and communication. You can start to feel the energy a few days before via messages & new information brought to awareness. And what is discovered, what is learnt, may bring discomfort & frustration. Depending on where Gemini sits in your personal chart, the best way forward is to digest what may strike a touch of instability & insecurities and figure out its origins & the whys/hows it affects you currently.

### Pluto Retrograde in Capricorn

With PlutoRx in Capricorn involved, strike back by empowering yourself with your deepest desires or letting go of the past and parts of you that hold you back. The dregs of the past can further cloud the Gemini air. Intuition and creativity are also part of the agents of change and renewal to set you in more positive thought and motion.

#### Neptune in Pisces

Neptune in Pisces is the potent influence on the NM. It brings about awkwardness and confusion. The idea is not to use force on the issues at hand. Rather, to write it out, explore it via music, a form of creative play and glide into it in gentleness and believe despite the confusion, you can walk out of the haze non-sensically(with your power of intuition), maybe even doing something off the wall with Uranus in Taurus in the mix of influence.

In essence, when planting new seeds in the area of life Gemini energy influences, stride in creativity, in intuitive power. If you find yourself in a rabbit hole, take your time to get to know that environment; even at its most absurd level, you may learn something that moves you into new curious territory and the challenges shake you into excitement.
