**Dating a Cancer Zodiac Sign and Why You Should Stay Away…**

Being with a Cancer and its need for familial bond is connecting to their body and flesh. They need and expect to grow with you deeply. If you are looking for something else, stay away. Cut both losses if independence and individuality are at the top of your priority list. They can become attached and clingy with tendencies to smother and give you ample attention.

**Warning:** Do you like the waters tranquil in your relationships? Unless there is vital earth energy to ground them or airy influence to rationalize their moods, they are emotionally moody AF. If they do not get the emotional response needed, as a cardinal sign, they will push and snap at you until they get their way. If you are moody yourself, the lack of balance can heighten and provoke hypersensitivity.

Cancer energy requires lots of comfort, feeling at home and internal nurturing. If you are up for the task, retreating or running off to their dark caves should not be taken personally. They are a hypersensitive bunch with strong emotions that run deep and, for better or worse, attuned to your own. It’s their way to soften themselves up again. Just make sure there is enough emotional communication and understanding. Laying it bare is a way to avoid hoarding ill feelings that can eventually turn them permanently cold.

#### Sun in Cancer

##### Cancer Moon

The Good:

They are the divine mothers and intuitively know what you need. They will cry with you, ensuring that even the slightest wound gets a sweet kiss of love. They extend that affectation to your friends. Anyone that enters a Cancer Moon’s home will feel warmth. Return the warmth as they will need it to replenish. The home and family are their domain. Add food to that mix, and you will see satisfaction. Show them how much you care with emotional gestures, and you’re (warmly) covered for a while. Being with a Cancer Moon romantically can be compared to having your needs met on a maternal, rooted level.

The Bad:

At its lower vibration, their moods can set you adrift, other placements like the Ascendent can placate this energy, but if needs are met, you’ll know. They are vulnerable to the energy of the environment. Claws can be drawn out of reaction. A Cancer will express exactly what they are thinking. It is a potent moon placement, with high tides of emotions. You can take advantage of the turbulence to clear the waters.

To help calm the tides, feed and nurture them emotionally and physically. They, too, need mothering with all that mothering they do themselves. A home cook meal, food that warms them, and showing them you are willing to swim in their sometimes tepid, sometimes torrential waters shows that unconditional support is returned.

The key is constantly communicating in sensitivity with a Cancer in Venus by stating your needs and boundaries–it helps with accountability.

One essential piece of advice is to avoid resolving a Cancer Venus issue by having solutions —that is all good and will definitely work at times, but what a Venus Cancer or any Venus in a water sign requires is to be given space to express what sometimes cannot be described in words but rather acknowledged by company and merely being present.
