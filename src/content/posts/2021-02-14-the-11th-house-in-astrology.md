## The 11th House in Astrology: Known as the area of friends, social circles and our aspirations—how we contribute to the evolution of society.

![The 11th House in Astrology](/images/uploads/2021/02/12-640x580.png)In the [5th House](https://astrowildchild.com/astrology-elements/the-5th-house-in-astrology/), we create who we want to be, how we want to shine and what part of our identity we develop to distinguish ourselves from the rest of the world. It’s a creative house showcasing our talents, what we produce, and what we can take pride in. In the 11th House, we integrate our 5th House creative self-identity into the social sphere and onto the bigger picture to strengthen, promote and expand.

The 11th is commonly known as the House of friends, social circles and our aspirations—how we want to improve the world and envision it evolving.

We contribute our talents and our ideas in the 11th House realm. By connecting with others, those of the same mind and interest, we explore our visions of an ideal world, to improve community with our sense of identity and ideas along with it. The 11th House expands on that identity into new possibilities. It’s where we go beyond our own needs and ego-self, where we become aware and understand our fundamental unifying force with others.

> No man is an island, entire of itself; every man is a piece of the continent, a part of the main. ~John Donne

It is the sphere of life naturally associated with the humanitarian sign of Aquarius, the genius planet Uranus and dutiful Saturn. The 11th House symbolizes that no man is an island; our very autonomy and brilliant ideas cease to exist without the breath of community. Integrating our unique selves into the broader picture gives further meaning and purpose. Ideally, what we contribute tightens, accelerates our connection and evolution as a collective.

The sign at the cusp colors the energy we put into the 11th House.

Fire signs bring dynamism, inspiration and creativity. Air signs will connect to this area of life on mind level, sharing ideas and concepts. Watery elements can heighten emotion, intuition and can get personally attached to others and a cause. There is a tangible nature to the earth signs in the 11th House, a steady placement focusing on results.

The planets show the pursuit of action and interaction within group settings. The general luck given by Venus and Jupiter creates optimism and ease in interaction, a seamless expression of the native’s hopes and dreams. The harmonious energy indicates the high potential to turn aspiration into reality.

Awkward Saturn can be stifled with pessimism and have a serious bent in social circles.  Being career-oriented in the 11th House, the line between professional associates and friends is strongly marked or the exact opposite is true, and the native’s limited time in other areas of life can spawn friendships in the professional atmosphere.

Pluto, the planet of transformation and power, can sway others, make historical changes, while there will be a need for constant renewal of friends or growth in dynamics throughout life.

Both signs and planets can also shed light on how one is perceived and received. Neptune in Sagittarius, can be popular, glamorized and can enjoy many friends, but can easily lose their own autonomy.

Mars in Gemini works on a surface level and is seen as social. It’s a high-performing energy that can also be pegged as being aggressive with words, easily making friends, but just as quickly abandoning them.

Others will sense the intensity and provocation of Mercury in Scorpio. The nature of Scorpio in this area of life gives the native a keen sense of awareness; there is a deep strategy combined with Mercury. Its penetrative nature will navigate the 11th House cautiously, and the energy can turn into paranoia on the extreme end. Its powers of intuition can manipulate the 11th House tides to a desired outcome.

In the 11th House, we turn our unique self-expression into purpose, connecting ourselves and our ideas with a larger group. It is the area of life where we find our community and contribute to society’s evolution.
