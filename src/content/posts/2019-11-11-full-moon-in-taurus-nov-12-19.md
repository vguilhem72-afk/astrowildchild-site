_**The Full Moon**_ sheds its light in the rhythmic sign of Taurus, giving clarity, completions & bearing fruit in an area of life. Taurus is sensible, it’s about material values & being in tune with the five senses. It is a ripe period to finish up goals concerning material wealth & general physical comforts, making way for new ones.

The Moon does well in Taurus, expressing its highest vibration, capable of calm nourishing support,  the perfect touch of emotional comfort. An emotive sensibility that can soothe the grating opposition happening with the Sun & Mercury in Scorpio.

Scorpio is about the internal world, deep intimate bonding, a sharing of resources. What we identify with, how we see things on a deeper level can now become discordant with physical comforts, material resources. In other words, outer reality vs. Inner desires.

Because both the Moon & Mercury(retrograde) are involved, logic & emotion can turn into jarring notes. The answer is in their shared feminine & intuitive qualities & in considering the emotional desires of two sides in a logical & practical way.

The upbeat flow of the Neptune in Pisces trine will soften, dissolve any other lingering harsh sounds, as it beams its idealistic & magical light onto a uniting solution. A cadence that will continue by the support of Saturn & Pluto in Capricorn, helping to find constructive, transformative & lasting results, keeping us in tune with the Full Moon’s melodic vibration.
