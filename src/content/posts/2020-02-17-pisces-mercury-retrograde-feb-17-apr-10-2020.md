If your headspace has been traveling out of bounds, your thoughts spacing you out or plans & schedules not gaining much traction, things can heighten once Mercury Retrogrades in Pisces.

Pisces Mercury feels like a retro period on its own. The only difference is when in actual retrograde, the journey turns inward. The next few weeks will be about reflecting, reviewing & re-editing the area of life Mercury travels in your chart. Whether it is on an personal or social level, affecting the material or beyond, being caught in the confusing web of uncertainty & delusions can create further entanglements with the influence of Neptune in Pisces & Mercury squaring Moon in Sagittarius right from the retro turn. Getting caught in Neptunes net can mean flights of fancy, a blurring of boundaries or elusive ideas expanding emotional vagueness.

Since there’s no avoiding the haze, step into it & let the gears of intuition guide you. As thoughts dissipate or elude, go with it. Allow imagination, sensitivity & compassion override logic. Fueling other parts of yourself may actually delight you, awaken dormant areas, even take you to a place of magic & wonder.

For those uneasy with limitless & emotional Piscean energy, rely on the trust that’s grounded on the practical past, faith levelled on reality or work, strategies & systems that have proven successful. Just make sure to create wiggle room & room for creativity.

Mars enters Capricorn on the 16th (today for some), another aid from an area of life that can make for decisive, grounded action, will & determination to help cut through the haze.

Be easy on yourself, use compassion & let your imagination ride the reflective retrograde dance.
