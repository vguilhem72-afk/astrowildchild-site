### Saturn and You

A picture of sliced mango comes to mind. Two halves cut from its pit. Both sides turn into a bowl-like shape that your hand’s cup snuggly, scoop up the soft sweet meat with a spoon, and enjoy. In my humble opinion, it’s the best way to eat a mango. I remember seeing the juicy flesh cut in the same way but with an added step. On each half, you cut smaller diced sections and when you turn it inside out, it blooms in segments.

It was the most wonderful thing I witnessed for some reason. I thought it was genius, to be honest (yet, I don’t ever prepare it that way). In fact, I peeled it in the beginning, then cut it into slices. Pretty time-consuming compared to the easier, more convenient two.

Saturn operates the same way in our lives. At first, the planet’s requirements feel laborious, seemingly robbing you of your time, attention and effort. As you move along the journey with the father figure in Astrology, you realize (usually in hindsight) each limiting and awkward experience was necessary for improvement and efficiency.

The more intimate your relationship with Saturn—which I liken to understanding life from a mature lens, synonymous with seeing your parents under that same lens with humility—is when you start to appreciate and respect his energy and stickler for order and discipline. Heck, I think there is something wondrous, cool about Saturn’s influential life force. But the essential energy is only recognized when you’ve created a bond with Saturn—with that part of yourself.
