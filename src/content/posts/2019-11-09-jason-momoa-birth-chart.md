## Jason Momoa Birth Chart: A Leo Sun Vibe, Scorpio Moon Depth Stirred by Uranus Eccentricities

![Jason Momoa Birth Chart Wheel](/images/uploads/2019/11/JasonMNatalC-640x709.png)As one of the biggest actors in Hollywood today,  Jason Momoa’s big personality definitely fits the role. Though his shining characteristics, comfortably displayed on screen, is attributed to his stellium in [Leo](https://astrowildchild.com/star-signs/leo-self-mastery/)–Sun, [Mercury](https://astrowildchild.com/astrology-elements/mercury-in-astrology-expression-and-interaction/), [Venus](https://astrowildchild.com/astrology-elements/venus-the-choices-we-make/), [Jupiter](https://astrowildchild.com/astrology-elements/jupiter-in-astrology-inherent-truths/), I’ve always thought of him in his Moon sign of [Scorpio](https://astrowildchild.com/star-signs/scorpio-the-super-healer/).

You can guess a Scorpio Moon by their penetrative, and sultry gaze. The power and depth in their eyes–a balance to his masculine Leo energy. Equally strong in loyalty and fixity, it shows his emotive(Scorpio) & intuitive(Leo) strength. Both signs of commitment, it is his Scorpio Moon which deeply bonds him with the past, with his mother & his roots—apparent in much of the media photos & videos with his mother & the connection he has with his hometown of Iowa + ancestral roots from Hawaii.

![Jason Momoa Birth Chart Short Read](/images/uploads/2019/11/aquaman-painting-1-640x791.jpg)

Under a prominent Leonian vibe, he wears a proud badge of a husband and father. Whenever asked about his wife, he is all praise and gratitude. His cubs being his pride, they run like a wild pack,  as seen in the adventures and shenanigans shown in his vlogs/posts for his initiatives [On The Roam](https://www.youtube.com/c/JasonMomoa) and his atypical production company Pride of Gypsies. An unconventional streak due to his native [Uranus](https://astrowildchild.com/astrology-elements/uranus-in-astrology-our-awakening-process/) conjunct his Scorpio Moon.  An eccentric expression going beyond his choice of outfits, his raw & rebellious spirit breaks the mold of the poised & refined Hollywood star.  However, he may not be the famous name now had it not been for Saturn conjunct North Node in Virgo to eventually temper his carefree spirit.

The North Node is our mission in life, our spiritual process. The sign it is in tells us what we must develop in this life to reach spiritual satisfaction. In the sign of [Virgo](https://astrowildchild.com/star-signs/i-want-to-do-this/), he is to learn consistency, to improve skills.  Saturn is maturity, lessons, discipline, goals and career. Saturn conjunct his NN may have caused Momoa to trip up through out his career life—it took him 20 years to get where he is—but Saturn delivers after time and hard work. Virgo is also known as the steward of the earth, while Saturn also rules past systems and current structures. A mission Jason Momoa evidently fulfills by his activism with the environment and its natural resources.
