## New Moon in Libra — 6-month Action to Harvest

First let’s clarify the difference between the 2-week cycle and 6-month growth. 

**The 2-week cycle:  the sprout.**

You plant the seed on October 10. By October 25-26 (Full Moon in Taurus),  the sprout breaks through the soil. You see the first sign of life. The first result. The first feedback on whether the seed took root.

But the Full Moon in Taurus illuminates your intention through a **Taurus lens**. That is self-worth, material security, what's tangible. It's not the same lens you planted the seed with. You planted in Libra energy:  relationships, harmony, attraction. Taurus checks whether the foundation is solid. That's a checkpoint, not the harvest.

**The 6-month cycle:  the bloom.**

The Full Moon in Libra on March 22 illuminates your intention through the **same Libra lens** you planted it with. Relationships, harmony, balance, what attracts you. It comes full circle. The seed and the harvest speak the same language. That's the completion.

### **How do the following Full Moon phases in different sign connect?**

Every 2 weeks the Moon passes through a Full Moon in a different sign. Each one checks in on your Libra intention from a different angle:

- **Full Moon in Taurus (Oct 25)** — is the foundation solid?
- **Full Moon in Gemini (Nov 24)** — have you communicated what you need?
- **Full Moon in Cancer (Dec 23)** — does it feel emotionally safe?
- **Full Moon in Leo (Jan 22)** — are you being seen and valued?
- **Full Moon in Virgo (Feb 20)** — have you done the practical work?
- **Full Moon in Libra (March 22)** — full circle. The harvest in the same energy you planted.

You don't WAIT 6 months. You work with every Full Moon along the way. Each one reveals a different layer of whether the intention is growing. But the 6-month Full Moon in the same sign is where it all comes together because it's the only one that matches the energy of what you originally planted.

**Also consider the size of the intention matters too** 

A small practical intention — "I'm going to have an honest conversation with my partner this week" — that's a 2-week cycle. Plant it at the New Moon. Harvest it by the Full Moon.

A bigger life intention — "I'm going to fundamentally change how I show up in relationships" — that's a 6-month cycle. It needs Scorpio's honesty, Sagittarius's faith, Capricorn's discipline, Aquarius's innovation, and Pisces's surrender before it can fully bloom. You can't do all that in two weeks.

**The simplest version:**

2 weeks = quick results, first feedback, one angle.

6 months = full circle, same energy, complete picture.

##### This is how it actually works in practice.

**You don't set a separate intention for every New Moon.**

You set ONE main intention at the New Moon that matters most to you. That's your anchor. Everything else orbits around it.

**Using my example:**

My anchor intention was planted at the New Moon in Libra in your 4th house on Pluto — release the old emotional patterns and build healthier foundations.

The subsequent New Moons aren't separate intentions. They're **natural extensions** of the same one. You don't have to consciously set them. Life does it for you:

- **New Moon in Scorpio (5th house)** — you don't sit down and say "now I'm setting a creative identity intention." What happens is that clearing the 4th house emotional patterns naturally opens up creative expression you'd been suppressing. The New Moon just highlights that it's happening.
- **New Moon in Sagittarius (6th house)** — you don't formally plant a "new habits" seed. What happens is that as the old comfort zones dissolve you naturally start replacing them with different routines. The New Moon just illuminates what's shifting.
- **New Moon in Capricorn (7th house)** — you don't consciously set a partnership intention. What happens is that the relationship dynamics naturally start changing because YOU changed. The New Moon just marks that transition.

**The practical approach:**

**Track one intention.** For me, it is my Libra 4th house Pluto intention. That's it.

**Use the Full Moons as check-ins.** Not as separate intentions. Just pause at each Full Moon and ask: how is my original intention showing up in this area of my life right now? One question. One reflection. No new seeds needed.

**Let the New Moons do their work passively.** The supporting seeds plant themselves because transformation in one house naturally ripples into the adjacent houses. You don't manage them. You notice them.

**Think of it like this:**

You throw one stone into a pond. That's your New Moon intention.

The ripples that spread outward. Those are the subsequent New Moons. You didn't throw six stones. You threw one. The ripples are natural consequences of the original splash.

The Full Moons are you checking different edges of the pond to see how far the ripple has reached.

**So the simplified practice is:**

1. **One intention** at the New Moon that hits you hardest personally
2. **Notice** what shifts in other areas naturally over the following months
3. **Reflect** at each Full Moon — how is the original intention ripening?
4. **Monitor** each New Moon and the changes naturally happening. 
5. **Harvest** at the Full Moon in the same sign 6 months later.
