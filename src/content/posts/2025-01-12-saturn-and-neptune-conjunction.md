You will hear a lot of negativity about the upcoming constricting [Saturn](https://astrowildchild.com/saturn-in-astrology/) and nebulous Neptune conjunction in Pisces. The first round will start in April 2025, and it will perfect in [Aries](https://astrowildchild.com/aries-in-astrology/) at 0° Feb 2026.

The low vibrating predictions involve conflict in certain areas of life that can throw you off balance, set confusion and blur all that you’ve been passionate about or working towards. I’m here to let you know there is a bright outcome once Saturn’s dance with Neptune wanes in March 2026. It is a blessing in disguise as each planet continues a dance with Pluto in Aquarius, and Neptune connects in harmony with Uranus in Gemini. The conjunction gives you a chance to different parts of yourself that increases your value in the changing transactional, social landscape.

Let’s break it down.

### **I’ll start with the first conjunction in Pisces and follow it up with Aries.**

Currently, Saturn is in Pisces, in Neptune’s territory. Wherever Saturn goes, it wants you to be realistic, work hard, create boundaries and put effort in what matters. In the sign of Pisces, you can apply that to your dreams, creative aspirations to make them reality, spirituality as a daily practice, or generally creating more flow in life, becoming emotionally intelligent, and developing compassion. 

For example, if Saturn is crossing your 7th house of relationship in Pisces, consider how boundaries can create a sustainable connection. You might want to work on your sensitivity, emotional status. It could be about getting real about the needs and expectations of others. Practice ways to improve relationships as a life-long habit. Saturn rewards you for discipline, tenacity and resilience. 

Come April, Saturn will move closer to Neptune, an opposite energy that expands instead of limits, dissolves instead of creates, and accepts instead of excludes. It works with reality vs. imagination. Neptune works subtly, requiring you to accept things as they are through faith and hope, believe in something higher, and experience it through trust, prayers, and miracles.

Slower-moving Neptune’s influence is much more potent than Saturn’s, especially since they’re in Piscean, Neptune-ruled waters when they first meet.

**So what does that mean?**

All the edges of your goals, what you’ve been working towards to improve and build on can start to lose its definition. There might be a feeling of inadequacy, confusion and insecurity. There will be a loss of control, maybe even authority, a position of power. Your Saturn edges and limitations can thin in Neptune’s haze. Foundations, structures might not be as solid. Your goals, bonds you’ve created, work you’ve built, possessions you attained or hold dear, and values you set will suddenly give new meaning, for some, in shocking ways. 

My mother was born with this conjunction, and her upbringing and experience exemplify the negatives about the influence. She is the youngest of four children. Her parents separated when she was a toddler. The conjunction happened in the sign of Libra—she experienced the pain of her parent’s relationship dissolving, and soon after, she also experienced separation from her siblings. When her mother married another man and had more children, her aunt adopted her, while her 3 siblings lived with other relatives. Her foundation crumbled, leaving her displaced and insecure.

Uranus was also transiting through her stellium of planets in Cancer (home), which included her Moon (home) and squaring the conjunction. She had a bit of help with Pluto in Leo sextile the conjunction. Clearly, her situation wasn’t off to a good start. However, the abrupt changes had a Uranus silver edge. Her mother’s sister took her in and raised her as her own. My mom talks about her second mother in the most loving light. She says her experience with her as a child was amazing. My mother had it the best compared to her siblings. Her aunt didn’t have any children until her son was born years later, giving my 3-year-old mother full attention and doting—my mother has a healthy sense of confidence.

The treatment she would never have gotten, as the youngest of four, and then displaced in rank when her biological mother had four more children and her father added another six. At the end of it all, my mom claims she was also well taken care of by her oldest sister while in college and out of all her siblings, she was the closest to one of her half-sisters on her mother’s side. As an adult and accepting mother, she highlights the positive impact of her childhood. The radical change in home, in the traditional family dynamic, eventually worked to her benefit. 

#### **So, what is the best move during the conjunction? (More on the benefits later.)**

Compared to the young child that was my mother, you have control of your situation and can learn to let go of where you don’t. When it comes to Neptune, it is about surrendering and knowing where your tethers are. 

To start, let go of what naturally falls to the wayside, including general Saturn things like boundaries, limitations, rules, the past, and old structures that aren’t for your highest good anymore.  

In Pisces: A more subtle effect— let the delusions, the superficial, unhealthy imaginings, negative emotions, addictions and the patterns that have held you back get washed away.

Then apply them to the house(s) under the influence and where your Mars sits and transits in your natal chart. 

**The Opposite House and Sign, plus Uranus as Tethers:**

When there’s too much energy in one area of life, balance can be found in the opposite house and sign.

On the other side of Pisces is Virgo and practicing discernment, improvement, and daily rituals that keep you reality-based. The magic (Neptune) is about being present in tasks, routines and enjoying them in a healthy way. The South Node will be in Virgo, so more than ever, finding balance in the Pisces Virgo axis of your chart is critical. 

What are the indicators of a balanced life under the conjunctions influence?

It includes enjoyment in your daily life. You’re not overwhelmed, there’s a spiritual touch or creative excitement to what you do consistently. Your moods don’t fluctuate; you don’t get pulled in every direction. 

Example: if the conjunction is happening in your 10th house, you’re letting go of expectations, past structures that no longer work, unrealistic goals and desires, and fanciful imaginings to reach the top or attain a pinnacle status. Your spiritual or artistic career can take on a new meaning. If it sets confusion or leads you astray, your fourth house of home, comfort, family origins, and body can be your lifeline. The fourth house naturally grounds too. Hang out with family more, make it a thing. Work to improve (Virgo) on what actually nourishes your body and soul. Virgo habits in the opposite 4th house can be the tethers in Neptune’s nebulous seas. 

**Uranus in Taurus Support**

**While Neptune in Pisces will be ahead within a 10\* orb to Saturn, Uranus Taurus will sextile Saturn starting late Feb 2025 in a tight orb until May 2025 before Saturn moves into Aries. Neptune, at this point, will be in Aries at 1°. Unlike the challenging aspect with Uranus in my mother’s chart, Uranus will support the conjunction, but it will require effort to make things work to your advantage.** 

Act (Aries) preemptively, make bold changes that appease Neptune, and look at the situation through different lens by sparking your imagination and creativity. Act on your values to grasp reality. Do things that keep you in your body, five senses, and earthly pleasures. You’ll find value when you stay present and take it one beat at a time. 

As a side note, Neptune has been in a harmonious dance with Taurus Uranus since June 2022 (within a 10° orb). A revolution in our spirituality, creativity, artistic pursuits and how we connect subtly. We are also connecting in new and innovative ways. We’ll feel the Saturn and Uranus evolution on an earthly, practical level. Should you choose to accept the mission, it liberates (Uranus) you on a soulful (Neptune) and reality-based (Saturn) level.

Now consider the natural revolution from Pisces to Aries: last sign to first sign — a rebirth is happening.

Regulations, limitations and censorship seem like the obvious scenarios when Saturn transits Aries, curbing the warrior signs’ desires, individual expression and inherent passionate force. But looking at the last time it was in Aries, there were also significant leaps and strides forward. The list below indicates seeds of new things that developed through the decades. Included on the list are the deaths of iconic and independent thinkers, the seeds they’ve since dispersed, and notable ideas and innovations that define the fabric of our lives to date. It’s both a reminder and a scrubbing away of what isn’t up to snuff so that the Aries’ robust energy is at its optimal in Neptune’s foggy territory.

When things fall apart, when confusion sets in, what are you left with? Your own faculties of will, courage, strength, and what coincides well with Aries in Neptune influence is intuition. Trusting your gut to achieve authority with strength that originates from humility will be part of the process.

The Conjunction between Neptune and Saturn in Aries will dissolve structures, rules, and ego-reality to find a balance within self that can bridge you to fine-tune your connection to the collective ideal.
