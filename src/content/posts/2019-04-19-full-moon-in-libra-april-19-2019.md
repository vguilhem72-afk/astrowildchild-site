The Full Moon will be tipping Libra’s emotional scales out of balance in the area of relationships. This can be the relationship with yourself, others, ideas or habits, etc. Falling in the last degree of the sign, we may feel the pressure of something coming to an end, if not bubbling to be set free. Wherever it is that Libra falls in your natal chart it is the area where we seek balance, associate the highest beauty and place diplomacy.

With full lunar rays in this area, we may just be given insight to something that has long been hidden and that is now calling for our attention. If your emotions have been seesawing from one side to the other, this lunar influence may just be the energy that helps you out of stagnation.

Staying in the middle will no longer have the same comforts and security as the scales will be further rocked with the opposition to Uranus in Taurus. The tipping point will come from the square to Pluto, the South Node & Saturn in Capricorn. You may want to hold onto something or someone, stick to old ways, old habits or continue to live in an existing—but perhaps outdated—structure. The square shakes your emotional pans to highlight what’s no longer in harmony & the chance to redefine a true sense of equilibrium & beauty.

Activate the sextile with retrograde Jupiter in Sagittarius to give weight to higher purpose, your own personal code, and replace the load with personal truths. Step back & throw currency to analytical and creative energy from Mars in Gemini & Mercury in Aries, the two planets being in mutual reception, harmonizing your thoughts and actions.

The energy around the Full Moon is certainly one of changes & tension, as it highlights what needs to be ushered out and invited in. The upsets & conflicts may be causing discord to certain areas of your life, but may just be the tall order needed to keep your emotional scales even keeled once again.
