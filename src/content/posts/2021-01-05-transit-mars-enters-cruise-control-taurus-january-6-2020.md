After six months of being in its home sign of fiery Aries, Mars finally shifts into cruise control Taurus. Things start to slow down, the speedometer of action dials back in patience. You’ll find yourself desiring to take the time to smell the flowers, enjoying  physical, earthly pleasures.

A great way to go about things to balance the squares and conjunctions Mars will be up against with the outer, power playing planets throughout the month. On the 13th, it will be about your personal values vs. duties you have towards another, this can include an authority figure or an established institution. Around the 20th, surprising news or events may have you act out irrationally or compel you to make rash decisions. Approaching the 23rd, challenges can involve your personal values against another persons belief system, or a battle within can occur between current values and where you want to see changes and growth.

From the wise words of Buddha, “ In the stillness, wisdom appears.” Aligning to the Mars Taurus energy can help you achieve this.
